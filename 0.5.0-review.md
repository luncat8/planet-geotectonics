# 0.5.0 review — the 3D planet view: a displaced icosphere, a translucent sea shell, and the runaway pile it exposed

What the draft asked for, what shipped, what the measurements say, and what only the owner
rig can settle. Written to stand alone. Measurements are from the Arena sandbox (2 cores,
node 22, no WebGPU adapter) unless a log is cited; the mesh and frame-loop numbers carry
over to a real machine, the pixel and ms budgets do not.

## 1. Scope

The ask (`0.5.0-draft-3d-render.md`): a 3D render of the planet; a fast, good-looking
elevation technique; a displacement slider; tessellation finer and smoother than the input
grid; a decision on whether the 3D needs the GPU engine or may also ride the CPU one; and
four hard constraints — elevation visible from oblique angles **and affecting the
silhouette**, the height map updatable **without rebuilding the mesh or allocating**, no
heavy PBR or waves, and a translucent water shell.

Chosen technique (five candidates weighed in `0.5.0-plan-3d-render.md` §0): a dense static
icosphere displaced radially per vertex from a per-frame height texture, normals from
screen-space derivatives, three fragment stages (land / water / rim). The silhouette is the
geometry, the topology is built once, a height update is one compute pass into a texture.

## 2. What shipped

### 2.1 The mesh (`js/render3d.js`, `Render3D.mesh`)

Unit icosphere, k midpoint-subdivision rounds with a dedup cache keyed on the sorted
endpoint pair. Counts `10·4^k + 2` vertices / `20·4^k` triangles, every position unit to
< 1e-5, every face non-degenerate and wound CCW outward (the back-face cull needs it).
Level-independent — one mesh per k, reused across every sim level and both engines.

Measured here (node, 2 cores):

| k | Vertices | Triangles | Positions | Indices | Build |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | 40,962 | 81,920 | 0.5 MiB | 0.9 MiB | 20 ms |
| 7 | 163,842 | 327,680 | 1.9 MiB | 3.8 MiB | 59 ms |
| 8 (default) | 655,362 | 1,310,720 | 7.5 MiB | 15.0 MiB | 300 ms |
| 9 | 2,621,442 | 5,242,880 | 30.0 MiB | 60.0 MiB | 1217 ms |

k = 8 is sub-cell at every offered level (≈27 km edge vs 223/112/56 km cells at L5/L6/L7),
and its build is control-change class (300 ms), never frame class.

### 2.2 The height texture and its per-frame update

`R32Float`, 2048 × 1024 (8.0 MiB) for **every** k, so the mesh never aliases the content and
a future resolution change needs no 3D work. `r32float` is unfilterable in core WebGPU, so
the bilinear filter is spelled out as four `textureLoad` taps in one `heightAt` helper — no
sampler in the bind group, exact f32 heights, u wrapping in the tap indices. The gather is
one compute pass, 256 × 128 workgroups = 2,097,152 threads (one per texel), reading the
**sim's own segment-final z** — `cellF` bound directly on the GPU engine (zero copy, zero
readback) or a per-frame `queue.writeBuffer` of a pre-allocated `Float32Array(V)` on the CPU
engine (40 KiB at L5, 160 KiB at L6, 640 KiB at L7 — measured `V·4`, where V is 10,242 /
40,962 / 163,842 cells). A NaN gap stores the marker −1e9; the vertex stage
clamps displacement at `Z_FLOOR` = −15 km, so the marker is a pit under the water shell,
never a hole. Land and water fragments read the same `heightAt`, so a coastline's colour and
geometry cannot disagree by a texel.

### 2.3 Water, rim, lighting

Water: the same mesh displaced by the constant `Params.sea` (the 0.3.5 display level, level
or volume mode, live), depth-ramped from the seafloor height, alpha `0.45 + 0.25·(1−dot(N,V))²`,
one Blinn glint, `src-alpha`/`one-minus-src-alpha`, depth test on, depth write **off**, drawn
after the land into a `depth24plus` attachment. Rim: `pow(1−dot(N,V),3)·SKY`, additive. Land:
the 2D relief ramp (sea-relative, 0.3.3 `zRange`) × Lambert from one fixed world-space light
× a limb term. Three terms, no IBL, no roughness, no noise, no animation — the water is flat.

### 2.4 Camera, input, inspector

Orbit camera (yaw, pitch clamped ±89.5°, distance 1.5–10, default 3.0, 40° fov), one
pre-multiplied `view·proj` plus the eye in a 96-byte uniform written per draw — a drag or a
slider moves bytes, never a pipeline, so **orbiting never defers a sim step** on either
engine (unlike the CPU 2D re-sample). Drag orbits, wheel zooms, and the column inspector
works over the 3D view: `Render3D.pick` intersects the pointer ray with the unit planet (the
ray normalized before the sphere quadratic) and the hit direction maps to uv through the
gather's own convention. A corner ray misses — pinned by test.

### 2.5 Both engines

Both, as the draft asked. GPU engine: the session borrows `GpuSim`'s device and binds
`cellF`/`LOOK` through its own bind group — no extra boot, no copy. CPU engine: a
render-only device (default limits suffice) plus the per-frame z upload; the sim is
untouched. No adapter → the toggle is disabled with the reason in the probe line and the 2D
map is unaffected. Engine switch, Resolution select, Reset and Load all re-init through one
hook (`rebootV3d`), releasing the old session first (the 0.3.4 leak lesson).

### 2.6 UI, HUD, URL

`3D view` checkbox on top of the layer buttons (which grey out while it is on); Adjust gains
**Displacement ×** (1–40, step 0.5, default 10, floor 1 — no terrain inversion) and
**3D detail** (k 6–9, default 8, a one-time mesh rebuild). The perf strip grew one reserved
`V3D` slot (`SLOTS` 5 → 6) carrying the session's own timestamp line at 2 Hz, silent when the
adapter lacks `timestamp-query`. Capture header adds `3d on`, then `disp 12x` / `k7` only when
non-default. URL: `?v3d=1&disp=12&k3d=8`. Off by default: no session, no device, no
allocation at boot; `bench.html` does not load the module at all.

### 2.7 The sim-side fix the rig exposed

Faithful displacement showed what the 2D colour ramp had clamped away: single columns piled
**thousands of km** of sediment (probed at 3768 km on the rig; 1173 km by 600 Myr headless),
because sediment riding buoyant crust never subducts. The fix is in the sim, not the
renderer: sediment counts as thickness in the collision barrier (`plates.js`, so a
sediment-loaded margin goes hard and collisions stop stacking) and in gravitational collapse
(it flows off over-thick margins), and a column accepts it only while its total thickness is
under the collapse regime — the excess underthrusts with the slab (`subductedSed`). No free
constant: the limit **is** `hCollapse`. A legacy pile relaxes in one frame. The WGSL deposit
closure mirrors it, so parity holds.

## 3. Measured results

Test profile, this sandbox: `node tests/run-all.js` → **PASS, 30 tests in 97 s**
(`ok render3d 174 ms`). Full-profile histories and everything GPU were not run here
(AGENTS.md: the owner-rig gate).

The draft's hard requirements, as pinned by `tests/render3d.js` on the stub device:

| Requirement | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Tessellation finer/smoother than the grid | counts `10·4^k+2` / `20·4^k`, unit positions, CCW-outward, dedup exact |
| No rebuild, no allocation | **1000 frames → 0 buffer/texture creations**; mesh, height texture, z scratch and draw uniform are the same objects afterwards; retained heap +5 KB, ArrayBuffers −2.3 MB (forced GC either side) |
| Frame contract | one encoder = gather → land → water → rim, all into the 3D target; `present()` is its own submit onto the canvas (the black-frame rule) |
| Live knobs | displacement, the 0.3.5 sea and the 0.3.3 relief range ride the 96-byte uniform per draw |
| Gaps cannot break the geometry | NaN → −1e9 marker in the pack **and** in the uploaded bytes |
| Release is exact | session buffers/textures destroyed, a borrowed `LOOK`/`cellF` never is; a detail change destroys only the mesh buffers |
| Inspector agrees with the picture | centre pixel hits the sub-camera point, a corner misses, four hit directions project back to their own pixel within 0.01 px |

Session footprint (allocated once per session; released on 3D-off, world rebuild and engine
switch):

| k | Mesh | + height 8.0 + target 8.0 + depth 8.0 + cellZ ≤ 0.6 | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 22.5 MiB | 24.6 MiB | **≈ 47 MiB** |
| 9 | 90.0 MiB | 24.6 MiB | **≈ 115 MiB** |

## 4. The honest gaps

- **No GPU in this sandbox.** The pixel asserts (centre drawn, corner clear, disp 40× widens
  the silhouette, sea −2 km moves the coast) live in `webgpu-smoke.html` §3D and the
  **≤ 5 ms per rAF** budget in the plan §9 is an estimate until measured on the rig. Both are
  owner-rig gates: `run-smoke.bat` / `run-smoke.command`, and an L7 GPU 20-step 3D-on drag
  strip plus an L5 CPU 3D strip.
- **Found and fixed while closing out:** `tests/render3d.js` existed but was **not registered**
  in `tests/run-all.js`, so the 0.5.0 test never ran in any profile. It is now the 30th short
  test (and the frame-loop pin above was added with it — object identity alone would not
  have caught a per-frame `createTexture`).
- The **3D renderer** adds no physics: it reads the sim's segment-final heights and writes
  nothing back, so the 3D-off page and the bench are the page and bench of 0.4.5. §2.7 is a
  different matter — it **is** a sim change, on the calibrated procedural world. The short
  profile accepts it (`erosion`, `collapse`, `split` pin it directly), but the release
  calibration baseline in `js/params.js` (9.01 cm/yr mid-epoch, 19.3 % continental, 12 plates
  at 1500 Myr) was **not** re-measured here: that is `tests/longrun.js` /
  `experiments/sweep.js`, i.e. the owner-rig full profile. If the sediment blanket moved those
  numbers out of the acceptance windows, the baseline comment is what must be re-committed.
