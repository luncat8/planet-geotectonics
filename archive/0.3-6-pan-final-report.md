# Pan under a running sim — what changed and why (2026-09-14)

The owner compared the two pan branches (`apply-pan-copy`, `old`) against main and set the
bar: the sim keeps running whenever the pointer is not moving, and resumption must never
wait for mouse-up. Main's drag (quaternion trackball, no pole clamp) is kept; the pause and
the resample cost come from the branches' good ideas.

## Behaviour

- **The drag never pauses the sim by itself.** `beginPan`/`endPan` no longer touch
  `playing`. The frame loop owns the pause: it defers a step exactly on the frames the view
  moved (`viewVersion !== shownVersion`) **and** the CPU engine must re-sample. A held-still
  pointer pauses nothing; a resting pointer resumes on the next frame, mouse up or down.
- **The GPU engine steps under the drag at all** — its shader already projects per fragment
  from the view uniform, so a view move costs a 32-byte uniform write.
- **Click/drag dead zone (4 px)** from both branches: hand tremor is still a probe click,
  and the dead-zone wobble is replayed into the drag (the first real move composes from the
  press position, so coalesced moves cannot drift the view).
- The view outlives a rebuild (unchanged, now pinned).

## Performance (from the branches)

- **Fast re-sample** (from `apply-pan-copy`): degree-11 minimax `atan2` (1.7e-6 rad error,
  three orders under the 3e-3 rad half-pixel margin), precomputed per-column/row screen-angle
  tables, and `atan2` for the latitude instead of `asin`. L5 re-sample: **45 ms → ~18 ms**.
  Identity stays bit-for-bit the lookup (parity rig intact); a 56° rotated view flips a few
  dozen of 524,288 pixels against exact trig, bounded and pinned in tests.
- **draw/paint split** (both branches' dirty/panned idea): `draw()` recolours the cell colour
  buffer, `paint()` re-samples the screen table and paints the last colours. A view-only frame
  is re-sample + ~3 ms paint — no recolour.

## Verified

- `tests/gui.js`: probe names the cell actually painted under it after a drag; 2 px wobble is
  still a click and moves nothing; holding the button still keeps stepping; the re-sample
  frame defers its step; the resting pointer resumes without mouse-up; the view survives an
  L5→L6 rebuild; the GPU engine submits a play under an active drag and the GPU renderer was
  handed the moved view; identity re-sample is bit-for-bit; paint does not recolor (state
  changed under it); fast-atan flips bounded; `atan2Fast` error bound.
- Eight mutations each caught by one of those pins (no resample gate, the old
  pause-on-down/resume-on-up, no dead zone, rebuild resets the view, paint recolors, GPU
  gated by view moves, paint never re-samples, view never reaches the GPU renderer).
- Full `tests/run-all.js` suite passes.
