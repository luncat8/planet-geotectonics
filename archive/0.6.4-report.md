without any campaign, painted from the record's anchored direction like the campaign's sets -
one painter, one kind filter, stale-and-rebuild rules unchanged; a running or preparing campaign
still owns the canvas, and the campaign view takes the layer over on finalize. A click that lands
on a painted marker (resolved against the projected positions the painter just cached, seam
wrapped, 7 px tolerance) pins that deposit's record at the top of the report panel and rings its
marker; a ledger-row click is the same selection; clicking either again releases it. The survey
keeps its full meaning underneath the pin. Below the report, a discovery list mirrors the ledger:
one row per find, sortable over found order / size / contained metal / top depth / confidence /
kind with a direction toggle, sharing the Kinds filter, capped at 200 rows with a count that
names the whole session. `Instruments.depositText` and `Deposits.principalOf` are new exports;
the list reuses them so no formatting path is duplicated.
