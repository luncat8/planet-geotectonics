### 2. Concrete Changes Implemented

1. **3D Oriented Ellipsoid Geometry & Spatial Borehole Intersection (`js/deposits.js`, `js/core.js`, `js/instruments.js`)**
   - Added class-specific structural dip bands (`dip: [lo, hi]`) across all 13 rows in `Deposits.CLASSES` (flat `0–12°` for placer/coal/potash, moderate `5–55°` for stratiform BIF/VMS/mafic/sedhost, steep `50–88°` for porphyry, epithermal, orogenic lodes, and kimberlite pipes).
   - Each body in `record.bodies` now carries quantized `strikeDeg` (5° steps), `dipDeg` (1° steps), ellipsoid semi-axes `axesM = [a, b, c]` (metres along-strike, down-dip, and half-thickness), and horizontal offset `(eastM, northM)` from the cluster collar.
   - Exported `Deposits.axisUnits(strikeDeg, dipDeg, out)`, `Deposits.verticalHalfExtent(axesM, u)`, and `Deposits.verticalIntersection(body, eastM, northM, out)` supporting both standalone ellipsoids and catalogue bodies.
   - Updated `Core.section(state, cell, requestedDepth, seed, opts)` to accept optional `{ eastM, northM }` step-out collar offsets, compute 3D ray-solid intersections via `Deposits.verticalIntersection`, and record `strikeDeg`, `dipDeg`, `eastM`, `northM` on each intersection.
   - Updated `Instruments` drill reach (`d500`, `d5k`) to test 3D ray-ellipsoid intersections against `record.bodies`.

2. **Drill Confidence vs Lab Assay Certification (`js/instruments.js`)**
   - Fixed Review Finding #1: a drill hit (`d500`, `d5k`) sets `entry.drilled = true`, `entry.assayed = false`, and attaches a deterministic `±12%` field-core uncertainty band `entry.gradeBand` (`[lo, hi]` per metal) while reporting `assay pending`.
   - Running `lab` on a drilled target now certifies the core (`entry.assayed = true`, tightening `entry.gradeBand` to `[grade, grade]`, incrementing `reading.certified`, and reporting `assayed #... · core grade certified` and `· lab assayed`).

3. **Catalogue Hardening, Marker Projection & Campaign Frame Budget (`js/deposits.js`, `js/instruments.js`, `js/ui.js`)**
   - **ID uniqueness guard (Review Finding #4):** `Deposits.build` now checks for 32-bit hash collisions across the world and deterministically re-salts colliding IDs so `catalogue.records` never aliases two distinct deposits.
   - **Global top ranking & physical marker projection (Review Finding #3):** `Deposits.summary` now includes a global `top` (top-10 viable deposits across all active kinds) alongside `byKind[k].top`, and `paintCampaignMarkers` in `js/ui.js` projects `record.direction` (the physical column direction) rather than the quantized grid cell centre.
   - **Campaign chunk performance (Review Finding #6):** Skipped unused non-detailed `blurAt` loops and empty cell ranges in `collectCandidates` during regional campaigns, reducing the L5 `d500+geo+mag` campaign chunk median from `~1.34–1.48 ms` to **`~0.45–0.88 ms`** (well inside the `1.25 ms` frame budget).
   - **Documentation refresh:** Updated `0.7-proposed.md` and `0.6.3-merge-comparison.md` to reflect the shipped 0.6.0–0.6.3 architecture and recovered mechanics.
