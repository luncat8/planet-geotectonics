## The "sim stops" bug: root cause found and fixed

Your stack trace was the whole answer:

```
OperationError: getMappedRange failed
  at pull (sim-gpu.js:712) ← roundTrip:789 ← step:811 ← play:826 ← webgpu-smoke.html:162
```

`pull` decided what to unpack by testing the **staging cache** (`if (stage.cellF)`), and the cache keeps buffers from an earlier, *wider* download. A light event round trip maps only `EVENT_READS`, then called `getMappedRange` on a cached `cellF` it had never mapped → `OperationError` → the play promise rejected → `ui.js`'s catch runs `setPlaying(false)`. That is literally "the sim stops until you press play again".

The trigger is any **full sync followed by an event cycle**: a probe click, Save, the deposit extract — or the **20 Myr checkpoint**, which downloads the full mirror. That's why it died at a fixed point in time and then ran fine for a while after a restart.

Fix: `pull` unpacks only what *that* transfer mapped. And `tests/gpu-stub.js` now enforces the map state, so `tests/gpu-play.js` **reproduces the identical stack headless at sim-gpu.js:712** before the fix and passes after. Engine errors now also reach `console.error` — the probe line was too easy to miss.
