## 1. Can the sim reach real historical plate positions? — the verdict, in `0.4.6-plan-plate-history.md`

**On its own: no.** That's a measurement, not an opinion: a forward run from the 250 Ma checkpoint plateaus at land-mask IoU **0.25–0.32** (0.4.5 §4). Reproducing the real plate sequence *emergently* is an inverse problem (assimilating mantle state from surface observables) — named as out of scope.

**With the kinematics prescribed: yes, exactly** — and the architecture already supports it. I verified in the source: `Columns.move` re-derives every column's world position from `body` + its plate's quaternion each frame (so a kinematic jump is legal, nothing advects on the grid), `Plates.integrate` advances `q` from ω, and `Sim.physics` already skips K10 on `prescribedOmega`. One thing it does *not* skip is `Events.cycle` — plate split/merge run even on a prescribed world, so the plan gates them.

**Reverse time: the two halves have opposite answers.**

| | reversible? | why |
|---|---|---|
| plate kinematics | **exactly** | rigid rotations form a group; the inverse is the conjugate quaternion — already in the code as `Quat.rotateInv`. One multiplication, no integration, no drift |
| the physics | **no** | erosion/sediment/contact/events all dissipate; subducted columns are deleted and ridge crust is created — negative `dt` cannot un-erode or un-subduct |

So the milestone ships **two modes**: **Mode K**, a lossless kinematic scrub to any epoch (the honest form of "reverse time", and it doubles as a deep-time slider on the 3D planet), and **Mode S**, the sim's own physics forward with ω(t) from the model.

**The blocker I found while grounding it** — historical packs have no real plate identity. Measured on the committed 250 Ma pack: plate 0 holds **24,687 cells and all 21,456 land cells** (the whole supercontinent as one plate), plates 1–8 are ocean Voronoi. §4 fixes it *without* new polygon data, by backtracking the modern pack's per-cell plate ids to the epoch. One external file is needed — the PaleoAtlas v3 `.rot` (Zenodo 10.251792, MD5 recorded in the plan). I could not fetch it: bash HTTPS is TLS-filtered here (PyPI works, the Zenodo zip doesn't), so it has to be dropped into `data/earth/` by hand.
