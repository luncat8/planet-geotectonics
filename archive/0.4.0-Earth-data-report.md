### 1. Collected Real Datasets (`data/earth/`)

1. **NNR-MORVEL56 Global Plate Kinematics (`data/earth/nnr-morvel56.json`)**
   - **Source:** Argus, Gordon, & DeMets (2011), NASA JPL / Rice University / UW-Madison, DOI: [`10.1029/2011GC003751`](https://doi.org/10.1029/2011GC003751).
   - **Coverage:** Complete data for all **56 tectonic plates** relative to the No-Net-Rotation (NNR) reference frame (25 MORVEL plates + 31 additional microplates from Bird 2003).
   - **Contents:** Exact Euler pole latitudes, longitudes, rotation rates $\omega$ in both $^\circ/\text{Ma}$ and $\text{rad}/\text{Myr}$, 3D Cartesian rotation axes $[n_x, n_y, n_z]$, rotation matrices $Q$, spherical surface areas (summing to $4\pi$ steradians), and canonical parent-plate ties.

2. **PB2002 Global Plate Boundary Model (`data/earth/pb2002_plates.json`)**
   - **Source:** Peter Bird (2003), UCLA, DOI: [`10.1029/2001GC000252`](https://doi.org/10.1029/2001GC000252).
   - **Coverage:** Metadata, bounding boxes, unit-vector seeds, and tectonic settings for all **52 plates**.
   - **Cross-reference:** Cross-mapped with NNR-MORVEL56 plates (how Africa maps to Nubia/Somalia/Lwandle; Australia to Australia/Capricorn/Macquarie; South America to South America/Sur).

3. **Ground-Truth Geological Calibration Dataset (`data/earth/calibration_points.json`)**
   - **Coverage:** **35 reference benchmark locations** across all key terrestrial and oceanic tectonic provinces:
     - *Archean Cratons:* Canadian Shield, Baltic Shield, Siberian Craton, Kaapvaal, Pilbara, Amazonian Craton.
     - *Collisional Belts & Plateaus:* Tibetan Plateau, Mount Everest summit, Altiplano, Alps.
     - *Continental Rifts:* East African Rift (Lake Tanganyika), Lake Baikal, Basin and Range.
     - *Flooded Continental Shelves:* Sunda Shelf, North Sea, Grand Banks, Sahul Shelf.
     - *Spreading Ridges:* Mid-Atlantic Ridge, East Pacific Rise, Southwest Indian Ridge, Gakkel Ridge.
     - *Abyssal Plains:* Western Pacific (Pigafetta Basin, ~160 Ma), Central Atlantic, Argentine Basin.
     - *Subduction Trenches:* Challenger Deep (Mariana Trench, $-10,920\text{ m}$), Tonga Trench, Atacama Trench.
     - *Major Sediment Depocenters:* Bengal Fan ($16.5\text{ km}$ sediment), Indus Fan, Mississippi Fan, South Caspian Basin.
     - *Oceanic Plateaus / LIPs:* Ontong Java Plateau, Kerguelen Plateau, Iceland.
   - **Precision:** Verified round-trip under the sim's K9 isostasy formula with a maximum residual error $< 0.005\text{ m}$.

4. **Authoritative Sources Registry (`data/earth/SOURCES.md`)**
   - Complete documentation of all official endpoints, persistent DOIs, primary HTTPS/THREDDS and Zenodo/PANGAEA URLs, MD5/SHA-256 checksums, resolutions, licences, and fallback procedures for ETOPO 2022, Seton/Müller Agegrid, GlobSed v3 (PANGAEA dataset 982339), CRUST1.0, and PALEOMAP PaleoDEMs (Zenodo: 5460860).

### 2. Offline Bake Pipeline & Initial Pack

1. **Python Bake Pipeline (`tools/earth/bake_earth.py`)**
   - Ingests real datasets (or calibration points + analytical spherical harmonic/IDW fallbacks);
   - Inverts K9 isostasy for $h_{\text{Fel}}$ and $h_{\text{Maf}}$ with multi-pass fixed-point relaxation for continental shelf transition coupling;
   - Calibrates vertical datum via area-weighted (latitude-cosine) binary search to achieve Earth's exact $70.81\%$ ocean fraction;
   - Quantizes layers to Little-Endian Int16/Uint8 and packages base64 banks into browser-ready JavaScript packs;
   - Runs with pure Python 3 standard library (no required third-party dependencies, accelerated by NumPy if present).

2. **Committed 1.0° Earth Pack (`js/data/earth-1deg.js`)**
   - Resolution: $360 \times 180$ cells ($1.0^\circ$ equirectangular);
   - File size: $509\text{ KB}$ base64;
   - Achieved ocean fraction: **$70.81\%$**;
   - Mean continental elevation: $+755\text{ m}$ (real Earth: $+840\text{ m}$);
   - Mean ocean depth: $-4076\text{ m}$ (real Earth: $-3680\text{ m}$);
   - Loads synchronously without network access in any `<script>` or Node.js environment.

### 3. Key Improvements Made to `0.4.0-Earth-map-plan.md`

1. **The Continental Shelf Inversion Rule (§1.1):**
   - Addressed the critical physical flaw where inverting $z < 0$ blindly as oceanic crust turns flooded continental shelves (e.g. Sunda shelf, North Sea, Grand Banks) into catastrophic $\sim 57\text{ km}$ thick basalt slabs. Continental crust flooded by sea water must be identified by geological crust type (CRUST1.0 or ocean crust age grid boundaries) and inverted for $h_{\text{Fel}}$ with $c_i \in [0.2, 1.0]$.
2. **Dual-Tier Plate Hierarchy (§4, §5):**
   - Defined `morvel25` (canonical 25 plates, collapsing microplates into parent plates via Argus et al. 2011 Table S3) to prevent microplates smaller than `minPlateCells` (100 cells at L5) from immediately collapsing on frame 1, while preserving `morvel56` for fine grids (L7/L8).
3. **Dual-Resolution Pack Architecture (§3):**
   - Established the lightweight $1.0^\circ$ pack (`earth-1deg.js`, ~520 KB) for instant loading, L5/L6 simulations, and automated test profiles, alongside the $0.5^\circ$ pack (`earth-05deg.js`, ~2.0 MB) matching L7 cell resolution.
4. **Mathematical Tie to 0.3.5 Eustatic Water Solver (§1.2, §7):**
   - Integrated the datum calibration directly with `Water.fromElevations(state.z, 1).level`, verifying that the baked pack's sea level lands inside the $58.6\text{ m}$ zero-bucket by construction so the 0.3.5 display level and volume sliders operate seamlessly on the real Earth map.
5. **Detailed Reconstruction Epochs for Milestone 0.4.5 (§8):**
   - Specified target epochs (250 Ma Pangaea, 200 Ma Central Atlantic rift, 150 Ma Gondwana breakup, 100 Ma Cretaceous, 66 Ma K-Pg, 40 Ma Eocene collision, 20 Ma Miocene, 0 Ma Present) with corresponding PALEOMAP PaleoDEM NetCDF sources and GPlates rotation models.
6. **New Pitfalls and Principles Logged:**
   - Documented shelf inversion physics, hierarchical plate safety, and monotone datum calibration in `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`.
