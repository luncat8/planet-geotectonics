### 1. The run itself

Nothing regressed against 2026-10-12-20-09: the vertex gather still matches
its CPU oracle to 9.90e-4 m (cellZ) and 1.06e-3 m (cellF) against the 0.05 m
gate, the lookup builds with 0 fallbacks / 0 clamps (32.0 MiB, max weight

### 2. The §14.4 gates (plan: gather ≤ 0.5 ms, complete 3D passes ≤ 5 ms)

| mode | gather | land | water | rim | draws (sum) | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hex | 0.065 | 0.262 | 0.262 | 0.262 | 0.787 ms | MET |
| vertex | 0.065 | 0.262 | 0.262 | 0.262 | 0.786 ms | MET |

Margins are 7.7× on the gather and 6.4× on the draws. Three readings of this
table were checked before acting on it:

- **The identical numbers are quantization, not a copied line.** Each pass is
  a single collect, the three draws carry the same k8 vertex load, and the
  values sit on a ~65 µs quantum (the k6 / 512×256 session in the same log
  still reads 0.000 — sub-quantum, exactly why it was never a gate number,
  and its gather reading 0 ticks against the gate session's 1 is jitter
  across that quantum). Even charging a full quantum of error to every pass,
  the draws stay under ~1.0 ms and the gather under ~0.13 ms: the MET does
  not depend on the fine digits. What the table does *not* show is a
  `vertex` premium — the record fetch and two extra taps cost less than one
  quantum at 2048×1024.
- **The world is L5, and that is the gate's configuration anyway.** The
  gather is a fixed 2048×1024 height-texture dispatch and the draws are the
  k8 mesh on a 2048×1024 canvas; the grid level changes the cell count
  behind `zAt`, not the texture, mesh or canvas the gates price.
- **§14.4 also asks for a timing distribution, and this log has one sample
  per mode.** That is now fixed in the smoke (below), but the switch does
  not wait for it: with 6-8× margins, no plausible spread around these
  samples crosses either gate. The distribution is regression record for
  the next rig run, not a missing precondition.

### 3. What this session changed

- **Default height mode is `vertex`.** `index.html` ships the Height select
  on Continuous heightmap; `js/ui.js` defaults `v3dHeight` to `vertex`,
  honours `?hmod=hex` as the explicit opt-out (an invalid `?hmod=` keeps
  the default), and the capture header now names `height hex` off-default
  instead of `height vertex`. A session still opens on `hex` while its
  lookup builds in row bands and swaps the frame it lands — that machinery
  is slice 3's, unchanged. `Render3D`'s own default stays `hex`: a session
  without records has no vertex pipeline, and that invariant is pinned.
- **The smoke's gate is a distribution.** `webgpu-smoke.html`'s `timeGate`
  collects 8 samples per mode (warm-up, then one collect per submit — the
  ring reads two submits back, so the samples walk in order) and prints
  median and max per pass, draws as their per-sample sum. The gate reads
  the **max**: the worst collected frame must fit. A missed gate still
  does not fail the smoke; an unmapped ring still does. Exercised here on
  SwiftShader (relative only, not filed): the smoke PASSes with both modes
  reporting `n=8` and the expected software MISS (hex gather med 51.2 /
  draws med 1283.8 ms; vertex 73.2 / 1365.9 ms — the record premium is
  visible where a frame costs seconds, invisible at hardware quanta).
- **Tests.** `tests/gui.js` pins the new default (select ships on
  `vertex`, `?hmod=hex`, invalid-`?hmod=` fallback, the session opening on
  cell samples and ending on the heightmap without touching the control,
  the header naming only `height hex`, the per-world rebuild). The stub
  suites are otherwise untouched; `node tests/run-all.js` short profile is
  green (38 tests).
