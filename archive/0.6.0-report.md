**What it does**
- **Frozen scenario:** the page has a new **Freeze catalogue** button. It pauses the world, pulls the GPU mirror if the GPU engine is on, and snapshots the geology with a checksum. It then generates the whole catalogue in timed chunks. The **Catalogue JSON** button stays disabled until the scan completes, and a reset cancels a running scan.
- **Generator** (`js/deposits.js`): the world is cut into 24,576 fixed tiles, independent of the sim grid. Each deposit is an ellipsoid with host, burial depth, strike/dip, ore tonnes and grades. Three families generate: volcanic-hosted massive sulphide (VMS), porphyry and orogenic gold.
- **Same bodies every way you ask:** each deposit is a pure function of seed, generator version and snapshot. Clicking tiles in random order, scanning in chunks, and exporting then re-importing all give byte-identical output. The sim state and its random-number generator are never touched.
- **Queries:** the code can list bodies near a point, scan the whole world in chunks, and find where a vertical drill hole cuts a body. The drill-hole function is groundwork for 0.6.1; nothing in the page uses it yet.
- **Export/import:** a foreign or version-mismatched export is rejected before anything changes.
- **New test** (`tests/deposits.js`, in the short profile): it compares point queries against a brute-force scan at the poles, cube corners and seams. It also checks that changing a potential doesn't reroll bodies elsewhere, plus resource formulas for % and g/t grades.
- **Renamed button:** the old "Deposits JSON" button is now labelled "Potential maxima JSON", since it is a diagnostic and not the catalogue.

**Caveats**
- **Priors are game parameters.** The sandbox has no internet, and the USGS percentile tables are images, so I couldn't fit them. Only two grade–tonnage correlations are published values; both are marked in the code.
- **Fitting the priors is the main open item,** and it would bump the generator version.
- **Mafic, basin and placer** have no family yet. The plan says to enable families only with real priors and tests.
- **Output size:** with the current tuning, a grown L4 test world gives about 5,000 bodies and 3 MB of JSON. `experiments/deposit-stats.js` prints counts and percentiles for tuning.
- **Cost on the owner rig:** whole-world scan time and JSON size at L5–L7 still need measuring there.

Natural next steps are fitting the priors, then 0.6.1 (instruments and synthetic core logs on top of the intersection function).
