# 0.3.3 follow-up: review of `0.3.3 adj`, the Tm slider, the erosion knee

Commit `e9c6318` ("0.3.3 adj") lands the live adjustment plan: the Adjust group's Cooling
switch and Mantle Tm slider, the Friction × and Erosion × scales promoted into the frame block
the `forces` / `erode` kernels already bind, the Relief range ramp on both renderers, the
`?tm/?cool/?fric/?ero/?relief` pre-fill, checkpoint VERSION 2 with `cooling`, `tests/adj.js`,
and the owner-rig capture `experiments/logs/full-test-2026-09-16-12-24.log` — which fails.

## 1. Review

What holds up: the wiring is one writer per control in both directions (`applyTm`/`applyAdjust`
write, `syncAdjust` reads back for a rebuild or a load), `applyAdjust` is bound to `input` so
every path — pointer, keyboard, pre-fill — lands on its consumer; the two promoted slots are
placed once (`GpuSim.FIN_*`), pinned against the generated WGSL by `tests/wgsl-struct.js`, and
`tests/gpu-play.js` pins 76 of 80 floats with the padding zeroed; the ramp rides the existing
per-draw uniform, so the relief slider costs no pipeline rebuild; `Mantle.setTm`'s re-anchor
(`Tm0 = Tfloor + (v − Tfloor)·exp(t/τ)`) is exact at t = 0 and mid-run, and `adjustReport`
prints only what differs from the compiled defaults.

Four findings, in order of weight:

1. **The new erosion default was shipped without the re-proof §3 of its own plan demands.** The
   q² intake multiplies the linear rate by `(z/zKnee)²`, and with the knee on the colour ramp's
   6500 m it ate the thickened crust: the L5 seed-7 release history falls to 14.1 % continental
   area at the 1500 Myr acceptance epoch, outside design §10.3's 15–40 % band. That is what
   `full-test-2026-09-16-12-24.log` reports, and the plan already said statistics drift without
   the re-proof "is a plan failure, not a tunable". Fixed by calibration, not by moving the band.
2. **`kEro` and `zKnee` are one degree of freedom.** `kEro·z·q² = (kEro/zKnee²)·z³`, so "the law
   is continuous with the linear one at the knee" is only a statement about the *pair*; the knee
   is the law's scale. `js/params.js` now says so, and `tests/adj.js` pins the scale as a literal
   (1.85 m/Myr at 1 km of relief) — a shape test written relative to `Params.zKnee` cannot see a
   knee moved quietly.
3. **The 2 Hz Tm follow was gated on focus, which a range input never gives up.** See §3.
4. **Two stale numbers were left behind**: `js/params.js` still quoted Phase G's release baseline
   (6.06 cm/yr, 18.8 %), which belongs to the linear law and the 4.5 Gyr profile; and
   `0.1.5-final-design.md` §7.3 — the normative design — still stated the linear intake. Both
   are updated (the design keeps the law, the plan keeps the calibration, `params.js` keeps the
   number the runtime reads).

Not a 0.3.3 issue, but visible while measuring it: the relief tail is not an erosion problem at
all. In the 300 Myr history the tallest columns hold 135–153 km of felsic crust — one cell each,
arc-saturated, z ≈ 21 km — because arc and collision accretion pile crust on a column that keeps
winning and `hCollapse` only spreads what is above 50 km; the linear law at the 1500 Myr epoch
still has a 63.8 km 99.9th-percentile land elevation. So "30 km peaks are prevented" can only be
a share statement (land above 20 km: 0.89 % → 0.28 %), never a cap, and it is why the elevation
ramp is a control rather than a constant. A cap on per-column accretion is 0.4.0 territory, and
this run does not open it.

## 2. The knee, measured

L5, seed 7, hot start, 1500 Myr at dt 0.1 — `experiments/erosion-knee.js`, one row per fresh
history, so the rows are comparable worlds. The linear-law row reproduces the committed
full-test log line for line (19.1 %, 9.57 % cratons, 9.05 cm/yr, 8 plates, 74/81), which is
what makes the rest of the table trustworthy.

| law | continents | cratons | mid speed | land > 2 km | land > 20 km | sediment | plates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| linear (0.3.2) | 19.1 % | 9.57 % | 9.05 | 69 % | 0.89 % | 1.00 | 8 |
| q², knee 6500 (as shipped) | **14.1 %** | 6.60 % | 9.62 | 85 % | 0.13 % | 0.78 | 10 |
| q², knee 9000 (new default) | 19.3 % | 10.06 % | 9.01 | 91 % | 0.28 % | 0.97 | 12 |
| q², knee 13000 | 19.3 % | 8.85 % | 9.18 | 90 % | 0.68 % | 1.31 | 13 |
| q², knee 18000 | 19.7 % | 9.93 % | 9.28 | 89 % | — | — | 12 |
| q², knee 26000 | 27.5 % | 14.43 % | 9.11 | 93 % | — | — | 13 |

9 km is the choice, not 13 or 18: it is the only candidate that returns *both* halves of the
draft's ask (low relief survives at 91 % of land, up from 69 %, and tall crust is cut 3× below
the linear law) while leaving the release statistics where Phase G put them — continents,
cratons, middle-epoch speed and the sediment inventory that drives the basin and placer
potentials within 3 %. The 13/18 km rows buy their continents by giving up the peak cap, and
26 km leaves the calibrated world altogether (27.5 % continents, 14.4 % cratons) — inside the
band, but no longer the planet that band was calibrated on.

## 3. The Tm slider

Symptom, from the page: move the slider and nothing happens until you click outside it, then it
jumps. Cause: the 2 Hz follow rewrote `tmInput.value` whenever `document.activeElement !==
tmInput`, and a range input keeps focus after the drag ends — so "while the user is editing"
lasted for the rest of the session, and the control (with its readout) stopped tracking the
cooling world. The dom stub could not show it: the test *set* `activeElement` to the element it
wanted protected.

Now a touch of the control arms a hold that the follow's own ticks retire (`holdTmFollow`,
2 ticks), and the page's own writes (pre-fill, rebuild, load) do not arm it. The thumb under the
pointer is still never rewritten, the control returns to the sim by itself a second later, and no
lost `pointerup` can leave a gate shut. `tests/adj.js` fails in both directions if either half
goes: "left alone while armed", and "resumes while still focused" — the second one is the
reported bug, and it reproduces it against the previous code.
