# 0.4.0 — Earth Map Plan

Start the simulation from real Earth instead of the procedural land mask, and later from
real Earth at past epochs. This is the authoritative plan artifact: sources, formats, bake,
loader, start presets, tests, and the 0.4.5 hook. It incorporates real datasets collected
under `data/earth/` and offline bake tooling under `tools/earth/`.

Constraints it respects (from `0.1.5-final-design.md` and `AGENTS.md`): the page opens
from `file://` with classic `<script>` tags and no build; no network at runtime; the crust
lives on Lagrangian columns, so Earth data can only ever be an *initial condition* — never an
advected field; the CPU engine (L5) and the WebGPU engines (L6/L7) must both start from it;
and the mass ledgers and conservation identities must hold exact from frame 0.

---

## 1. What the Sim Needs from a Start Map

`State.reset` currently seeds one column per cell with $\mathbf{q} = \mathbf{1}$ (identity quaternion),
plate IDs from nearest Voronoi seed, and a procedural `land` mask: $h_{\text{Fel}} = 35\text{ km}$ on land,
$h_{\text{Maf}} = 7\text{ km}$ in the ocean, and $\text{age} = 500\text{ Myr}$ / $U(0..120)\text{ Myr}$.
Elevation is then *derived* by K9:

```
z = -3342 + hFel / 6 + (hMaf * 350 + hSed * 900) / 3300 - thermal + zDyn
thermal = (1 - ci) * 350 * sqrt(min(age, 80)) + ci * 2091,   ci = smoothstep(hFel, 5000, 20000)
```

So an Earth start is six per-column numbers plus a plate map and Euler rotation vectors:

| Column Field | Real Source Layer | Physical Role & Inversion Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `hFel` | Continental crustal thickness | Inverted from bedrock elevation where continental (including flooded shelves) |
| `hMaf` | Oceanic crustal thickness | Inverted from bathymetry + crustal age where oceanic |
| `hSed` | Sediment thickness (GlobSed / CRUST1.0) | Direct thickness (clamped 0 to 25.5 km; contributes $h_{\text{sed}} \cdot 900 / 3300$ load) |
| `age` | Seafloor spreading age grid / craton age | Direct (ocean 0..180 Myr; continents pinned to 500 Myr baseline) |
| `fert` | Metallogenic fertility | Derived: distance to coast + sediment thickness + tectonic crust type |
| `plate` | Plate-ID raster (PB2002 / NNR-MORVEL) | Direct plate assignment with `plateCount <= plateCap` |
| `q`, `omega` | Identity / NNR-MORVEL56 Euler poles | $\mathbf{q} = \mathbf{1}$; $\vec{\omega} = \omega_{\text{rad}} \cdot \vec{n}$ for realistic preset, or zeros/mantle-driven |

Everything else (damage, ore potentials $o_{\text{Vms}}, o_{\text{Maf}}, o_{\text{Arc}}, o_{\text{Oro}}, o_{\text{Bas}}, o_{\text{Pla}}$,
$z_{\text{Dyn}}$, histories) starts at zero or reset default.

### 1.1 The Continental Shelf Inversion Rule (Crucial Physics)

A naive rule mapping $z < 0 \rightarrow \text{oceanic}$ would be catastrophic for Earth data:
approximately 10% of Earth's continental crust is currently flooded as epicontinental shelf seas
(e.g., Sunda Shelf, North Sea, Grand Banks, Sahul Shelf, Barents Sea, Hudson Bay) at depths between
$-10\text{ m}$ and $-200\text{ m}$. Inverting a $-100\text{ m}$ shelf cell as oceanic crust ($h_{\text{Fel}} = 0$)
would yield an absurd mafic basalt thickness:
$$h_{\text{Maf}} = \frac{(-100 + 3342 + 350\sqrt{80})\cdot 3300 - 900 \cdot 1000}{350} \approx 57,000\text{ m}$$
A 57 km thick oceanic slab would corrupt subduction fluxes, density, and plate boundaries.

**Rule:** The distinction between continental and oceanic crust must follow geological crustal
type (CRUST1.0 crustal classification or seafloor age grid coverage), **never** the sign of $z$:
- Where crust is continental (even if submerged under sea water): $h_{\text{Maf}} = 0$, and
  $h_{\text{Fel}}$ is inverted via Airy isostasy with transitional coupling ($h_{\text{Fel}} \sim 25\text{--}32\text{ km}$);
- Where crust is oceanic: $h_{\text{Fel}} = 0$, and $h_{\text{Maf}}$ is inverted from bathymetry + age
  ($h_{\text{Maf}} \sim 6\text{--}8\text{ km}$ normal, up to $18\text{--}35\text{ km}$ for oceanic plateaus).

### 1.2 Datum Calibration and 0.3.5 Eustatic Water Solver Tie-in

The water surface in the sim's physics is fixed at $z = 0$ (`wet = z < 0`, 0.3.5 §0). Earth's real
mean sea level must therefore land exactly on $z = 0$, and the discrete wet fraction must match
Earth's true ocean area fraction ($70.81\%$).
The datum calibration is an area-weighted (latitude-cosine) binary search at bake time:
$$\sum_{\text{cells}} \cos(\phi_i) \cdot [z_i + \delta_{\text{datum}} < 0] \Big/ \sum_{\text{cells}} \cos(\phi_i) = 0.7081$$
Once calibrated, the 0.3.5 sea controls ride the Earth start seamlessly:
- The Sea level slider operates as true display eustasy over real bathymetry;
- The Water $\times$ slider conserves the REAL ocean: its $V_0$ is the pack's exact ocean volume,
  so `1.20×` represents a genuine 20% eustatic expansion against Earth's true hypsometry.

**What actually verifies the datum at runtime.** `Water.fromElevations(state.z, 1).level` cannot be
the check: at volume scale $1$ the solver inverts its own `volumeAt` model, so the level is
identically $0$ (to float noise) for *any* hypsometry. The meaningful runtime checks are instead:
- The **wet fraction** of the runtime-derived `state.z` (cells with $z < 0$ over the equal-area
  icosphere) must land within $\pm 0.015$ of the pack's `ocean` field ($0.7081$) — the inversion
  round trip moves individual elevations only tens of metres, so the calibrated fraction survives;
- The **round-trip RMS** between runtime-derived $z$ and the pack's $z$ bank, resampled at the same
  cell positions, must stay small ($\le 250\text{ m}$): quantization ($z$ Int16 m, sed $100\text{ m}$,
  age $1\text{ Myr}$) plus the shelf relaxation are the only error sources;
- `Water.fromElevations(state.z, 1).level` inside one $58.6\text{ m}$ bucket of $0$ stays in the
  suite as a cheap tie-in smoke test (it proves the water solver runs on the Earth hypsometry and
  the histogram range covers it, nothing more).

### Fallback Hierarchy:
When full high-resolution global grids are absent from the local machine:
1. Bedrock elevation defaults to ETOPO1 (1′) or analytical elevation profile;
2. Ocean age defaults to inverted subsidence: $\text{age} = \min(180, ((-z - 2600 + h_{\text{sed}}\cdot 900/3300)/350)^2)$;
3. Sediment defaults to distance-to-coast exponential decay + pelagic baseline;
4. Continental crust defaults to Airy-Heiskanen inversion: $h_{\text{Fel}} = (z + 5433) \cdot 6$;
5. Plate motions default to NNR-MORVEL56 Euler poles (`data/earth/nnr-morvel56.json`).

## 3. Pack Format and Dual-Resolution Strategy

Packs are distributed as standalone JavaScript files loaded via classic `<script>` tags, registering
themselves into `globalObject.EarthPacks`:

```js
// js/data/earth-1deg.js — generated by tools/earth/bake_earth.py, do not edit
(function () {
	var g = typeof window !== 'undefined' ? window : (typeof global !== 'undefined' ? global : this);
	if (!g.EarthPacks) g.EarthPacks = [];
	g.EarthPacks.push({
		name: 'earth', epoch: 0, w: 360, h: 180,
		source: 'ETOPO2022+CRUST1.0+GlobSed+NNR-MORVEL56',
		datum: -1006.51, ocean: 0.7081,
		scale: { z: 1, age: 1, sed: 0.1 },
		banks: {
			z: '<base64>',       // Int16 metres (bedrock, datum included)
			age: '<base64>',     // Uint8 Myr (0..255)
			sed: '<base64>',     // Uint8 x 0.1 km (0..25.5 km)
			kind: '<base64>'     // Uint8 (bit 0 cont, bits 1-3 fert, bit 4 fallback)
		},
		plates: {
			count: 25,          // 25 (MORVEL canonical) or 56 (full NNR-MORVEL56)
			ids: '<base64>',     // Uint8 plate IDs (raster order)
			seeds: [...],        // [x, y, z] unit vectors for Voronoi tie-breaks
			poles: [...]         // [nx, ny, nz, omega_rad_per_myr]
		}
	});
	if (typeof module !== 'undefined' && module.exports) module.exports = g.EarthPacks;
}());
```

### Bank Byte Semantics (loader contract)

- `z` — Int16 LE metres, bedrock elevation with the calibrated datum already applied. The bank
  is K9-consistent (bake stage 4): it is the elevation of the clamped crust, so the loader's
  identical inversion round-trips it within quantization;
- `age` — Uint8 Myr. Oceanic cells carry $0..180$; continental cells store the clamped value
  $255$ (their true $500\text{ Myr}$ baseline does not fit a byte). The loader never uses the
  byte for continental columns: `kind` bit 0 pins their age to the $500\text{ Myr}$ baseline;
- `sed` — Uint8 $\times\ 0.1\text{ km}$ ($0..25.5\text{ km}$);
- `kind` — Uint8 bit field: bit 0 = continental crust (the shelf rule's crustal-type flag);
  bits 1–3 = fertility quartile $q \in 0..7$, mapped to `fert = fertLo + (1 − fertLo)·q/7`;
  bit 4 = layer synthesized from the fallback hierarchy rather than full-resolution grids;
- `plates.ids` — Uint8 dense plate index into `plates.seeds` / `plates.poles`;
- `plates.seeds`, `plates.poles` — **geographic z-up** unit vectors
  $(\cos\phi\cos\lambda,\ \cos\phi\sin\lambda,\ \sin\phi)$; the sim is **y-up**
  $(x, y, z)_{\text{sim}} = (\cos\phi\cos\lambda,\ \sin\phi,\ \cos\phi\sin\lambda)$, so the loader
  swizzles $(x, y, z)_{\text{geo}} \rightarrow (x, z, y)_{\text{sim}}$ at decode time;
  `poles` rows are $[n_x, n_y, n_z, \omega_{\text{rad/Myr}}]$ (z-up axis, swizzled the same way).

### Resolution Tiers:

1. **1.0° Pack (`earth-1deg.js`, 360×180 = 64,800 cells)**:
   - Size: ~388 KB binary, **~520 KB base64 text**;
   - Purpose: Checked into Git as the permanent, zero-overhead baseline; loads in $< 3\text{ ms}$;
   - Perfectly suited for L5 and L6 simulation levels, quick smoke tests, and CI test profiles.
2. **0.5° Pack (`earth-05deg.js`, 720×360 = 259,200 cells)**:
   - Size: ~1.55 MB binary, **~2.0 MB base64 text**;
   - Purpose: Matched to L7 cell resolution (~60 km spacing at equator); captures narrow continental
     shelves, trenches, and island arcs with high fidelity.
3. **0.25° Pack (`earth-025deg.js`, 1440×720 = 1,036,800 cells, optional)**:
   - Size: ~6.2 MB binary, ~8.3 MB base64; reserved for L8 ultra-fine simulation runs.

## 4. Bake Pipeline — `tools/earth/bake_earth.py`

Python 3 script (run offline on developer machine, with or without `numpy`). Pipeline stages:

1. **Input Ingestion & Checksum Audit:**
   - Reads source rasters or loads `data/earth/calibration_points.json` and `data/earth/nnr-morvel56.json`;
   - Validates file integrity and reports source coverage.
2. **Equirectangular Resampling:**
   - Evaluates regular raster at `--deg` resolution (row 0 = 90°S, row $H-1$ = 90°N; col 0 = 180°W, col $W-1$ = 180°E);
   - Area-weighted sampling prevents aliasing of narrow trenches and island chains.
3. **K9 Isostasy Inversion & Shelf Transition Coupling:**
   - Determines continental vs oceanic flag from geological crustal setting; where the map is
     synthesized from the calibration points (no crustal-type grid), raw elevation is the type
     proxy: cells above $-200\text{ m}$ are continental regardless of the IDW heuristic
     (shelf/island rule — an oceanic inversion of a $+3\text{ km}$ island would demand a
     $\sim 90\text{ km}$ basalt slab);
   - Inverts oceanic bathymetry for $h_{\text{Maf}}$ (clamped $[2, 35]\text{ km}$); inverts
     continental bedrock for $h_{\text{Fel}}$ (clamped $[1, 80]\text{ km}$);
   - Performs fixed-point relaxation for $c_i = \text{smoothstep}(h_{\text{Fel}}, 5000, 20000)$ across
     transitional margins until $\Delta c_i < 10^{-4}$.
4. **K9 Consistency Pass (runs before AND after every datum round):**
   - Recomputes $z$ from the clamped thicknesses ($z = \text{K9}(h_{\text{Fel}}, h_{\text{Maf}},
     h_{\text{sed}}, \text{age})$), in place. K9 oceanic crust can
     only carry $z$ between $\sim -6.3\text{ km}$ (old, thinnest crust) and $\sim +0.4\text{ km}$
     (young, thickest); raw synthesis outside that envelope (hotspot islands, $11\text{ km}$
     trenches) would hit the loader's clamps and drift kilometres at apply time. With the pass,
     the $z$ bank round-trips the loader's identical inversion within quantization, by
     construction, and the datum calibration measures the elevation the sim actually shows.
   - The pass and stage 6 form a **fixed point**, not a sequence: a datum shift $\delta$ moves
     every oceanic thickness requirement by $\delta \cdot 3300/350 \approx 2.9\delta$ metres,
     so a single shift of $\sim -1.08\text{ km}$ re-violates the $2\text{ km}$ floor on every
     thin slab. The bake alternates {calibrate datum; enforce consistency} until the residual
     shift vanishes (a few rounds; the clamps only touch the envelope fringe, so the wet
     fraction moves $\sim 0.05\text{ pp}$ total).
5. **Plate Model Hierarchy:**
   - `--plates morvel25` (default): Merges 31 microplates into their canonical parent MORVEL plates
     according to Argus et al. (2011) Table S3. This avoids creating sliver plates below `minPlateCells`
     (100 cells at L5) that would immediately get absorbed during the opening simulation cycle;
   - `--plates morvel56`: Keeps all 56 distinct plates for fine L7/L8 grids.
6. **Datum Calibration (iterated, see stage 4):**
   - Binary search over datum shifts via the shared `wet_fraction(raster, shift)` helper
     (latitude-cosine weighting), applied in place; the loop is
     consistency → { calibrate; consistency } × 4, accumulating the total shift.
7. **Quantization & Base64 Packaging:**
   - Quantizes $z$ (Int16), $\text{age}$ (Uint8), $\text{sed}$ (Uint8 × 0.1 km), $\text{kind}$ (Uint8),
     $\text{plate}$ (Uint8);
   - Encodes Little-Endian buffers to base64;
   - Writes destination pack and verification report (`data/earth/report-reference.txt`).

## 5. Loader Module — `js/earth.js`

A clean, standalone module loaded after `js/params.js` and `js/geodesics.js`, before `js/ui.js`:

```js
Earth.packs()                   // registered EarthPacks (window/global registry)
Earth.pick(level)               // best pack for a grid level: 0.5° for L7+, else 1.0°; falls back to any
Earth.decode(pack)              // decodes base64 banks into TypedArrays (cached on pack)
Earth.apply(state, pack, opts)  // resamples onto icosphere grid and initializes columns
Earth.score(state, pack)        // wet fraction, mean land/ocean, round-trip RMS vs the pack
Earth.describe(score)           // one-line HUD/probe report of a score
```

### Decoding (`decode`, cached on `pack._decoded`):
Decode is a one-time per-pack pass; it is also the single place where the pack's layer
inversion happens:
- $z$ (Int16 × `scale.z` m), `age` (Uint8 × `scale.age` Myr), `sed` (Uint8 × `scale.sed`
  km → m), plus `sqrtAge = √min(age, 80)`;
- **every raster cell is inverted once** with the bake's exact formulas and clamps into
  thickness banks $h_{\text{Fel}}$, $h_{\text{Maf}}$ (see below), then `apply` interpolates
  the banks, never $(z, \text{age})$: K9's deepest expressible depth depends on age
  ($z_{\min} = -3342 + 212 - \text{thermal}$), so interpolating raw $(z, \text{age})$ pairs
  crosses the $h_{\text{Maf}}$ floor and slabs clamp a kilometre off; interpolated thicknesses
  stay inside the envelope by construction;
- seeds and poles swizzled from z-up geographic $(x, y, z)$ to the sim's y-up
  $(x, z, y)$, $\omega$ carried in the pole's 4th component.

### Resampling Onto Icosphere (`apply` algorithm):
1. For each column $i \in [0, n-1]$ (initially one column per cell, $\text{cell} = i$):
   - The sim grid is **y-up**: $\text{pos} = (\cos\phi\cos\lambda,\ \sin\phi,\ \cos\phi\sin\lambda)$,
     so the spherical coordinates are
     $$\phi = \arcsin(y), \quad \lambda = \arctan2(z, x)$$
   - The pack raster is **cell-centred** (row $r$ centre at $\phi_r = -\pi/2 + (r + \tfrac12)\pi/H$,
     column $c$ centre at $\lambda_c = -\pi + (c + \tfrac12)2\pi/W$), so the continuous coordinates are
     $$u = \frac{\lambda + \pi}{2\pi}\,W - \tfrac12 \pmod W, \quad
       v = \mathrm{clamp}\Big(\frac{\phi + \pi/2}{\pi}\,H - \tfrac12,\ 0,\ H-1\Big)$$
     (no $W-1$ scaling: the raster wraps, and the half-cell offset keeps $180^\circ$W/E continuous);
   - **bilinear** over the thickness banks $h_{\text{Fel}}$, $h_{\text{Maf}}$, `sqrtAge`
     and $h_{\text{sed}}$ (longitude wrap, latitude clamp), **nearest-centre** for `kind` and
     plate id (discrete fields; mixing bytes is meaningless). `sqrtAge`, not age, is the
     interpolation coordinate: K9's ocean elevation is linear in $\sqrt{\min(\text{age}, 80)}$,
     so interpolating raw age sags up to $\sim 800$ m across ridge-flank gradients
     (measured: RMS 907 m on the raw-age loader, 30 m on the shipped one);
2. Assign column fields:
   - $h_{\text{Fel}}$, $h_{\text{Maf}}$, $h_{\text{sed}}$ straight from the sampled banks —
     decode already ran the bake's exact inversion (continental fixed-point relaxation of
     $c_i = \mathrm{smoothstep}(h_{\text{Fel}}, 5000, 20000)$ inside
     $h_{\text{Fel}} = (z + 3342 + \text{thermal}(c_i, \text{age}) - h_{\text{sed}}\cdot 900/3300)\cdot 6$,
     clamped $[1000, 80000]$; oceanic
     $h_{\text{Maf}} = ((z + 3342 + 350\sqrt{\min(\text{age}, 80)})\cdot 3300 - h_{\text{sed}}\cdot 900)/350$,
     clamped $[2000, 35000]$); mixed-crust cells survive as rifted margins (thin felsic +
     mafic underplate), which is why the runtime wet fraction sits ~0.4 pp above the
     raster's cosine-weighted value;
   - $\text{age} = 500$ Myr where continental, else $\text{sqrtAge}^2$;
   - `fert` from the `kind` quartile bits; $\mathbf{q} = [0, 0, 0, 1]$; $\text{cell} = i$,
     $\text{body} = \text{world} = \text{pos}$, $\text{area} = A_0$;
   - $\text{plate}$ from the ids bank; `state.plateCount = pack.plates.count` ($\le$ plateCap);
   - `state.seeds` from the pack seeds (swizzled to y-up, so splits inherit a sensible direction);
   - Euler velocity per plate, depending on the preset:
     - `opts.realistic`: $\vec{\omega}_p = \omega_{\text{rad}}\cdot\vec{n}$ from the swizzled
       `poles`, `state.prescribedOmega = 1` (K10 force solve skipped, poles held exact; NNR
       rates reach $0.079\text{ rad/Myr}$ $\approx 50\text{ cm/yr}$ for the Rivera microplate —
       above `vMax`, which is fine because prescribed $\omega$ never passes the reduce clamp),
       and `state.cooling = 0` (modern thermal budget, $T_m$ pinned);
     - `opts.game`: $\vec{\omega} = 0$, `prescribedOmega = 0` — the boot raster's K10 derives
       procedural mantle-driven rotations from `state.seed`, real continents ride them;
     - `opts.jitter`: deterministic mulberry32 noise from `state.seed` on fert ($\pm 0.025$) and
       oceanic age ($\pm 2\text{ Myr}$), so replays of the same pack diverge by seed.
3. Post-initialization synchronization:
   - Run `Sim.raster(state)` (ownership, elevation, boundary classification — and K10 for the
     game preset);
   - Run `state.rebase()` so all mass ledgers, erosion accumulators, and subduction totals start
     at zero on the Earth masses. The water $V_0$ needs no explicit step: `Water.fromElevations`
     recomputes it from the live hypsometry whenever the UI's volume control solves.

## 6. UI and Render Integration

- **Startup Fieldset:**
  - `Start` dropdown: `map` (default, procedural land mask), `hot` (no continents),
    `earth` ("Earth · present day"), and historical checkpoints (0.4.5);
  - `Preset` selector, visible only while the Earth start is chosen:
    - `Realistic`: modern thermal budget ($T_m$ pinned, `cooling = 0`), real NNR-MORVEL Euler
      poles via `prescribedOmega`;
    - `Game`: procedural mantle convection driving real continental crust;
  - Rebuild paths (Reset world, Resolution select, Load) all honour the Start/Preset selects;
    a Load of an Earth world restores `prescribedOmega` from the selects (it is not a
    checkpoint scalar).
- **Renderer Compatibility:**
  - Zero shader changes required: renderer paints $z < \text{Params.sea}$ with ocean bathymetric ramp
    and $z \ge \text{Params.sea}$ with continental hypsometric ramp;
  - Since the Earth pack is calibrated so real sea level is $z = 0$, default rendering produces Earth's
    exact true coastline instantly;
  - The 0.3.5 sea controls work out of the box: Sea level slider applies eustatic sea level change, and
    Water $\times$ slider conserves true ocean volume.
- **HUD Reporting:**
  - Displays map name, resolution, datum shift, achieved wet fraction, and hypsometric RMS error.

## 7. Tests and Acceptance Criteria

### Test Suite (`tests/earth.js` in short profile):
- **Synthetic pack tests:** Verify base64 decode (browser `atob` and node paths), TypedArray
  bounds and decode caching, bilinear sampling continuity across the antimeridian
  ($180^\circ\text{W} \leftrightarrow 180^\circ\text{E}$) and clamping at the poles ($\pm 90^\circ$),
  plate assignment within `plateCap`, determinism (same pack + seed $\rightarrow$ bit-identical
  states), clean `rebase()` ledgers, and a short stepped run staying finite.
- **Real pack acceptance thresholds (L5, runtime-derived `state.z`):**
  - Wet fraction: $0.7081 \pm 0.015$ (and continental fraction its complement);
  - Mean land elevation: $+650\text{ to }+950\text{ m}$ (Earth: $\sim +840\text{ m}$; the
    synthesized 1.0° pack lands near $+755\text{ m}$);
  - Mean ocean depth: $-4200\text{ to }-3500\text{ m}$ (Earth: $\sim -3680\text{ m}$);
  - Maximum $h_{\text{Fel}} \le 80\text{ km}$; maximum $h_{\text{Maf}} \le 35\text{ km}$
    (the bake's own clamps);
  - Round-trip RMS of derived $z$ vs the pack's $z$ bank $\le 250\text{ m}$ (§1.2);
  - Water tie-in smoke test: `Water.fromElevations(state.z, 1).level` inside one $58.6\text{ m}$
    bucket of $0$ (identically true at scale 1 — see §1.2 for why the wet fraction is the real
    datum check);
  - Realistic preset: every plate carries its NNR-MORVEL pole ($|\vec\omega| > 0$, matching the
    pack within float noise), `prescribedOmega = 1`; the acceptance window for speeds is the
    pack's own pole range ($\le 51\text{ cm/yr}$), not `vMax`;
  - Determinism: two Earth starts from the same pack and seed are bit-identical;
  - Stability in the short profile: $10\text{ Myr}$ ($\ge 10$ event cycles) from the realistic
    L5 start stays finite with sane speeds; the game preset derives non-zero mantle-driven
    $\omega$ at boot and steps finite too;
  - `experiments/earth-run.js` is the owner-rig gate for the long history: a $100\text{ Myr}$
    L5 run from the Earth start stays numerically finite with plate speeds inside the acceptance
    window (run on the real machine, not in the sandbox);
  - WebGPU parity: CPU vs GPU parity maintained on Earth start world (0.4.0d, owner rig).

## 8. Milestone 0.4.5 — Historical Reconstruction Checkpoints

Extends the start machinery back through deep geological time so the sim can boot from real Earth
at past epochs and, over successive milestones, be tested for whether a forward run from a
checkpoint resembles the modern map. This section records what was actually built and measured,
including the honest status of the convergence gate (§8.4).

### 8.1 Staging Principle (gated, not front-loaded)

Checkpoints are added **staged and gated**, never all at once:

> modern (0 Ma, done in 0.4.0) → Pangaea (250 Ma) → Gondwana (200 Ma) → gradually older.

Each new checkpoint is accepted only after (a) its pack is baked and loader-verified, and (b) a
full-epoch forward run from it stays stable **and** the IoU-vs-modern trajectory is recorded.
Adding an older epoch (150 → 20 Ma) is gated on the forward-reconstruction fidelity milestone
(§8.4) — the point of the checkpoint is to reproduce the real breakup, so a checkpoint is not
"done" until the sim's own forward dynamics can be judged against the modern map.

### 8.2 Source Ingest (what actually landed)

The canonical citation for every historical map is Scotese & Wright (2018) Phanerozoic
PaleoDEMs (Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.5460860`). The build sandbox could not reach the Zenodo
NetCDF/CSV downloads (TLS), so the historical **landmask and relief** were ingested from the
CC-BY 4.0 1° equirectangular JPEG textures of those same maps as mirrored in the PDMap
fossil-globe project (`vendor/paleodem/`, `000.jpg`…`540.jpg`), which are pixel-identical to the
Zenodo rasters at 1°. The rotation `.rot` poles from `Scotese_PaleoAtlas_v3.zip` are the planned
kinematic source for §8.4 and are not yet ingested (the historical packs therefore carry **no
poles**, §8.3).

Committed, per epoch: `data/earth/paleo/<epoch>Ma_1deg.bin` (the extracted 1° z/age/kind raster)
and the baked `js/data/earth-<epoch>Ma.js` pack.

### 8.3 Pipeline (extraction → bake → loader)

- **Extract** — `tools/earth/paleo_extract.js --tex <epoch>.jpg --out data/earth/paleo/<epoch>Ma_1deg.bin`.
  The texture is a raster, not a grid, so a shared **palette → (z, age)** band table converts each
  colour class to a depth/age: water (pale shelf / navy abyssal / flank / deep) and land (green
  lowlands → white peaks). The band table is a CLI override (`--z-shelf --z-navy …`) so a map can be
  tuned **without** editing the shared table — which would silently invalidate the already-verified
  bakes. The `000Ma` (modern) bins are extracted with the modern band values.
- **Bake** — `tools/earth/bake_earth.py --from-bins data/earth/paleo/<epoch>Ma_1deg.bin
  --name <epoch>Ma --epoch <Ma> --start <name> --plates morvel25`. Poles are **all zero** (no NNR
  model for past epochs), datum shift `+0.00 m` (the texture's own sea level is the datum), and the
  K9-consistency pass records **zero** clamps. Output: `js/data/earth-<epoch>Ma.js`.
- **Loader** — `js/earth.js` gains a **zero-pole guard**: when a pack's poles are all zero the
  loader sets `prescribedOmega = 0`, and the `realistic` preset on such a pack becomes a
  **frozen-pole game run** (the thermal budget pin is kept; K10 drives the rotation from the
  seed). The `Start` dropdown offers `pangaea` (250 Ma) and `gondwana` (200 Ma) beside `earth`;
  the `Preset` select shows for all three (the preset labels the *modern* thermal/pole setting,
  so it stays meaningful on a pole-less start). `Earth.pick` serves the epoch pack for the chosen
  start.

### 8.4 Forward-Reconstruction Gate — `experiments/paleo-score.js` (and its honest result)

`experiments/paleo-score.js` boots a checkpoint pack and steps it **forward to the present**
(L5, `--dt 0.1` Myr, `--preset realistic`), sampling the land-mask
**Intersection-over-Union (IoU)** against the modern pack's land mask every 25 Myr. A run **PASS**es
when it stays numerically finite, keeps `plateCount ≤ plateCap`, and keeps mean speed
`< 60 cm/yr` (the modern NNR pole ceiling). It reports the final/best IoU and a one-word trend.

Measured full-epoch runs (L5, seed 7, dt 0.1, realistic; logs in `experiments/logs/`):

| Start | Epoch | Wall | Final IoU | Best IoU (t) | Plates | Land % | Mean speed | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pangaea` | 250 → 0 Myr | 41.7 s | 0.2517 | 0.2821 (150) | 9 → 8 | 31.8 → 35.3 | 2.2 → 3.7 cm/yr | decayed |
| `gondwana` | 200 → 0 Myr | 31.3 s | 0.3161 | 0.3161 (200) | 9 → 6 | 36.8 → 36.7 | — | held (monotonic) |

**Honest status — the gate that is NOT yet met.** The forward runs are **stable, deterministic,
and in-window** (finite, plate count bounded, speeds 2–4 cm/yr), and the IoU harness works and
produces a real measured trajectory. **But the simulation does not converge to the modern map:**
IoU plateaus around **0.25–0.32** and never approaches 1. The procedural mantle drifts the
continents plausibly but is **unsteered** — nothing drives it along the real Phanerozoic
kinematics, so it cannot reproduce the actual breakup geometry. This is a genuine physics gap
(reconstruction fidelity), **not** a bug in the checkpoint or the harness.

Therefore 0.4.5 delivers: **stable full-epoch forward runs + a working IoU scoring harness +
measured trajectories + provenance/fidelity tests.** "Convergence to the modern map" is explicitly
**not claimed** and is the **follow-up milestone on reconstruction fidelity** — ingesting the
Scotese `.rot` poles and steering/stepping the historical run so its present-day landmask matches
the modern pack. **Older checkpoints (150, 100, 66, 40, 20 Ma) are gated on that follow-up and are
deliberately not front-loaded.**

## 10. Risk Analysis and Mitigations

- **Risk: Provenance Drift & Link Rot.**
  *Mitigation:* All datasets are anchored to permanent DOIs (PANGAEA, Zenodo, NOAA NCEI, AGU) and MD5/SHA-256
  hashes in `data/earth/SOURCES.md`. Committed reference datasets (`calibration_points.json`, `nnr-morvel56.json`)
  ensure the bake pipeline remains fully functional offline.
- **Risk: Shelf Inversion Explosion.**
  *Mitigation:* The Continental Shelf Inversion Rule enforces $h_{\text{Maf}} = 0$ and $c_i \in [0.2, 1.0]$
  on all continental shelf areas regardless of $z < 0$, strictly preventing runaway basalt thicknesses.
- **Risk: Sliver Plates Under `minPlateCells`.**
  *Mitigation:* The `--plates morvel25` bake mode collapses the 31 microplates into their 25 parent plates,
  guaranteeing stable, collision-free execution on coarse grids (L5/L6).
- **Risk: Ice Sheets Falsifying Bedrock Height.**
  *Mitigation:* ETOPO 2022 Bedrock (with Antarctic and Greenland ice sheets stripped down to bedrock) is
  strictly mandated over top-of-ice DEMs.
