# 0.6.0 — Instruments and the survey click

The first increment of the deposit prospector (`0.6.x-plan-deposit-prospector.md`). It ships the
exploration loop itself: a selectable instrument set, a click that reports what those instruments
can actually measure in one cell, the deposits they can find, the session ledger that keeps a
discovery stable, and the overlay that draws both on the map.

It also ships `js/deposits.js` with the parts the instruments need — the anchor, the stable id, the
thresholds and the catalogue cache — but not the economics: a record at the end of 0.6.0 carries
`kind, cell, anchorKey, id, potential, host, ageMyr, epochMyr, lat, lon, plate, top` (depth below
the surface). *Size, grade and viability arrive in `0.6.1-plan-deposit-catalogue.md`*, and every
panel line this file specifies has a slot for them already printed as `—`.

## 1. What an instrument is

An instrument is three numbers and two predicates, not a minigame:

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `reach` | the deepest top-of-deposit it can sense, metres below the surface (or below the seafloor on a wet cell) |
| `coverMax` | the greatest sediment cover it works through; above it the target is blind |
| `footprint` | how far from the clicked cell it senses: `0` = the clicked column only, `1`/`2` = that many rings, `-1` = the drainage below the cell |
| `kinds` | the bitmask of record classes it can respond to at all |
| `detect` | the signal-to-noise limit at which a record is *found* (see §3) |

### 1.1 The seven instruments

Order in the table is the order in the UI. `class` keys are `Diag.ORE_NAMES` plus `iron` (derived,
0.6.1).

| id | name | reach | coverMax | footprint | kinds | character |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `obs` | Field observation | 50 m | 5 m | 1 ring | all | cheap, qualitative; outcrop, structure, gossan, stream pans |
| `geo` | Stream-sediment geochemistry | 0 (surface) | 200 m | 3 hops downslope | all | basin-average anomaly; smears, cannot resolve grade |
| `mag` | Gravity + magnetics | 3 km (depth-integrated) | ∞ | 2 rings | `mafic vms arc iron` | shape and depth-to-source, no assay |
| `gpr` | Shallow reflection / ground radar | 1 km | ∞ | 0 | `basin placer iron` | sediment stack, depth to basement, placer horizons |
| `d500` | Shallow drill 500 m | 500 m | ∞ | 0 | all | assay of the interval; the only cheap quantitative read |
| `d5k` | Deep drill 5 km | 5000 m | ∞ | 0 | all | full column + assay; feeds the core (0.6.2) |
| `lab` | Assay + isotopes | sample | ∞ | 0 | all | refines a record already found; upgrades confidence |

Notes that keep the set honest:

- `obs` is the only instrument that needs *exposure*: a deposit is visible to it only if
  `top ≤ 50 m` **and** the cell is land (`z > 0`) or a wet seabed with `hSed ≤ 5 m` (a modern
  VMS mound). Its reading is an index, not a grade.
- `geo` walks the routing graph (`state.low`, up to 3 hops) *downhill from* the clicked cell and
  averages the blurred potentials over the cells it drains, weighted by `mobile` load; the dilution
  factor is the count of contributing cells. It is the only instrument that sees a deposit whose
  body sits in a neighbouring cell but drains into this one — and it pays for that by reporting a
  basin mean, so a 0.9 potential next door reads as 0.3.
- `mag` integrates over depth: it responds to `hMaf`, `damage`, `belt` and the mafic-linked
  potentials, and reports a depth-to-source bucket (250 m) rather than a grade.
- `gpr` and `mag` work through cover; `obs` and `geo` do not; `d500`/`d5k` are below it by
  definition and report the cover they passed through.
- `lab` is the only instrument that cannot find anything on its own. Selected alone on an
  unsurveyed cell it reports an empty reading, which is the honest answer.

### 1.2 Cost

There is no money in 0.6.x. The cost of an instrument is *information* — reach buys depth, assay
buys grade, footprint buys breadth, and only the two drills produce a quantitative number. The
plan keeps a single session counter (`surveyed cells`, `found records`, `viable records`) so the
player and a later economy increment have the accounting; nothing reads it for gating.

Rejected: simulated instrument time or a per-instrument cooldown. It would make the demo wait on a
timer to hide a cost model that does not exist yet; when the economy increment lands, the counter
is the hook it prices.

## 2. What a click reports

`mapClick` keeps its existing meaning — probe the column, with the same hover/follow behaviour —
and additionally runs `Instruments.survey` when at least one instrument is checked. Hover never
surveys. With nothing checked, the page behaves exactly as today.

The panel (`#prospect`, in the control column, monospace, fixed-width columns) is filled in this
order:

```
cell 4,812 · L5 · 43.1°N 12.7°E · continental · z 480 m · sediment 240 m · surveyed d5k,lab
readings
  obs   gossan 0.31 · structure: belt · drainage pans: 2 flakes (mobile placer 0.04)
  geo   basins 7 cells · anomaly index  vms 0.02  mafic 0.11  arc 0.34  oro 0.28  bas 0.00  pla 0.05
  mag   hMaf 3.1 km · damage 0.22 · magnetic index 0.41 · depth to source 1250 m
  gpr   sediment 240 m · reflectors 3 · depth to basement 240 m · placer horizon @ 180 m
  d500  hole 500 m · sand 240 m · granite 260 m · assay Cu 0.18% Zn 0.02% · no intersection
  d5k   hole 5000 m · sand 240 m · granite 3100 m · gabbro 1660 m · assay Cu 0.42% Mo 0.011%
        intersections: #3f2a arc 0.42 %Cu, 0.011 %Mo, 620-1180 m, 62 Mt, indicated
  lab   refines #3f2a: inferred → indicated · age 312 ± 8 Myr · byproduct Ag 1.1 g/t
found deposits (3)
  #3f2a  arc      indicated  620-1180 m  62 Mt  0.42 %Cu 0.011 %Mo   viable
  #8c11  orogenic inferred   1.8-3.2 km  18 Mt  2.9 g/t Au           sub-economic (depth)
  #b703  placer   inferred   0-40 m      —      0.11 g/t Au         needs d500 to size
```

Rules the panel obeys:

- **A reading is always printed; a record is only printed when found.** `readings` show the
  instrument's own number for every checked instrument, including the ones that see nothing (a
  barren cell is a result — `no reading above background`).
- **Assay is the only place the true potential appears**, and only within the hole's reach. This is
  the "actual concentrations data" the draft asks for, and it is why drilling is the decisive tool.
- **A record found without a drill says so.** Any field the instrument cannot measure is `—` (or a
  range, when the instrument brackets it), never a false precision; 0.6.1 fills the slots.
- Values are quantized (S1) before they are printed: potentials `1/255`, depths 50 m, ages 1 Myr.
  The panel can therefore be compared line-for-line between two runs.

## 3. Detection: find, confidence, hysteresis

A record is a candidate for finding when it is inside the instrument's footprint and reach and
`cover ≤ coverMax`; the *finding* test is a deterministic signal-versus-noise comparison:

```
signal[k] = q255(potential) / 255 · sizeGain[k] · kindGain[instrument][kind]
noise     = Instruments.noise(seed, record.anchorKey, instrument)     // 0..1, integer hash
found     = signal ≥ detectLimit[instrument] · (0.7 + 0.6 · noise)
```

- `sizeGain` is `1` in 0.6.0 (no tonnage yet) and becomes the tonnage factor in 0.6.1; the shape of
  the test does not change, only the multiplier's source.
- `kindGain` is `1` for a favoured kind and `0.5` for a class the instrument only responds to
  indirectly (e.g. `mag` on an arc porphyry: the magnetic high is real but not diagnostic).
- `detectLimit` is per instrument and per kind family: `obs` 0.45, `geo` 0.25, `mag` 0.30,
  `gpr` 0.25, `d500`/`d5k` 0.10 (a drill that reaches a record *finds* it), `lab` — (never finds).

`Instruments.noise` mixes `(seed, anchorKey.x, anchorKey.y, anchorKey.z, instrumentId)` with the
integer avalanche mix `Contact.hash` uses, but keyed on the anchor and the instrument instead of
the cell and the frame. Same cell, same instrument, any frame: the same number. Two records in the
same cell differ because their anchors differ.

**Confidence** is the honest part of the report and rises only with evidence:

| level | label | requires |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `inferred` | remote sensing only (`obs`, `geo`, `mag`, `gpr`) |
| 2 | `indicated` | ≥ 2 independent instruments whose `found` tests both fired, or one instrument plus `lab` |
| 3 | `measured` | an intersecting `d500`/`d5k` hole (the assay brackets the interval), optionally refined by `lab` |

`lab` upgrades exactly one level per survey and never to `measured` without a drill. A record's
confidence never falls within a session (S5): once measured, the ledger keeps it measured for that
anchor.

**The ledger** (`Instruments.Ledger`, page-local, never checkpointed):

```
{
  coverage:   Uint8Array(grid.V),      // OR of instrument bitmasks used on each cell
  cells:      Uint32Array + cellsN,    // the surveyed cell indices, for the overlay
  found:      [ { key, id, kind, cell, anchorKey, confidence, epochMyr, firstSeen } ],
  byCell:     Int32Array(grid.V).fill(-1)   // bucket head into found, chained by next
}
```

- A `found` entry is written once. On every later survey its `confidence` is the maximum ever
  reached, its `cell` follows the record, and it is **retired only** when the record is gone *and*
  the cell's blurred potential has fallen below `0.7 × depositMin` — the hysteresis that stops a
  deposit flickering out of the player's notebook when the field crosses a bucket boundary.
- Re-association (S5): when a merge or terrane rebase changes an anchor, a stale entry is matched
  to the new record with the same `kind` whose world position is within 1.5 cells; on a match the
  entry's key and anchor are rewritten and the confidence carries over. No match ⇒ the entry is
  retired by the rule above.
- The ledger is rebuilt from nothing on reload and cannot be saved: exploration is a session
  activity, and the checkpoint format is untouched. (The deposits themselves are in the world; the
  next player can find them again — that is the point of S1–S3.)

## 4. The overlay

Everything new that is drawn goes to one new canvas, `#markers`, absolutely positioned over the map
canvases (`inset: 0; pointer-events: none`), sized to the map panel. Drawing happens in the UI after
either engine has blitted, so **no WGSL source, no render pass and no parity harness changes in
this increment** — `js/render.js` gains only the pure `MapView.project` helper — the deliberate
constraint from the umbrella.

- `MapView.project(out, x, y, width, height, dir, viewQ)` is added beside `MapView.direction` in
  `js/render.js`: it applies the inverse view rotation to a world direction and returns screen
  pixels. It is the same maths `Renderer.updateViewLookup` runs per pixel, run the other way for
  one point, and it is what makes the overlay engine-neutral (and 3D-ready later).
- **Deposit markers**: a 5 px diamond at the record's projected position, coloured by kind (the
  six ore-layer hues, plus grey for `iron`). Filled = passes the class screen; hollow = a record
  that does not (0.6.1 fills the screen; in 0.6.0 every record is hollow). A dimmer dot marks a
  `found` entry whose anchor has not been re-matched yet.
- **Survey coverage**: cells in `Ledger.cells` draw a 2 px translucent dot in the cell's kind
  colour, so a survey history reads as a scatter rather than a map of claims.
- **Budget**: markers are drawn only for cells in view, capped at `MARKERS_MAX = 4000`, ranked by
  contained metal when it exists (0.6.1) and by potential before that; the panel prints
  `markers 1,208 / 6,410 (capped)`. The overlay redraws when the frame loop has redrawn the map
  (a `dirty` or view change) or when the ledger or the visible kind set changes — never on its own.
- **3D**: the overlay is hidden while `v3d.on`. Projecting markers into the 3D camera is a separate
  piece of work with a real cost at k8/k9; it is named here as a hook, not faked in 2D. The panel
  and the exports work in 3D unchanged.

## 5. UI, URL, capture

- New fieldset `#prospect` (Prospect · explores the clicked cell) below Startup, with the seven
  checkboxes (in the §1.1 order, `input name="inst"`), the `Map markers` checkbox (`#depmap`,
  default on), `Build viable map` (`#viable`, wired in 0.6.2), `Export deposits` (`#exportdep`) and
  `Export core` (`#exportcore`, 0.6.2). The results panel `#prospect` is a `<pre>`, scrolled to a
  fixed `max-height`, so a long core cannot push the controls off screen.
- Prefill and capture follow the page's existing convention: `?inst=obs,geo,d5k&depmap=0` on load;
  the capture header gains `prospect inst obs,geo,d5k · markers off` **only** when something
  differs from the default (nothing checked, markers on) — the `adj` policy.
- The existing `Deposits JSON` button (today `Extract.json`, min 0.15, per-class 12) is replaced by
  `Export deposits`, which writes the catalogue (0.6.1 format). `js/extract.js` keeps its
  primitives; its `deposits`/`json` wrappers are deleted in 0.6.1 once nothing calls them, per the
  no-leftovers rule. `tests/ores.js` and `tests/browser-scripts.js` are updated in the same commit
  as the removal.

## 6. Tests

`tests/instruments.js` (new, short profile; builds a small world, plants known columns, calls
`Instruments.survey` directly):

1. **Reach and cover gating** — a record at 800 m is invisible to `d500`, visible to `d5k`, and
   invisible to `obs`; a 300 m-deep record under 400 m of sediment is invisible to `obs` and `geo`
   and visible to `gpr`/`d5k`.
2. **Footprint** — `obs` finds a record one ring away; `d500` does not; `geo` finds it only when
   the routing graph drains the record's cell into the clicked one (the placer rig's cone is
   reused: erode it and check the pan signal appears downstream, not upstream).
3. **Determinism of noise** — same `(seed, anchor, instrument)` twice ⇒ identical; two anchors in
   one cell ⇒ different; the value does not change when `state.t` or `state.frame` changes.
4. **Detection limit** — a 0.2-potential record yields a reading but no `found` entry for `obs`; a
   0.9-potential one does.
5. **Confidence ladder** — `geo` alone ⇒ inferred; `geo`+`mag` ⇒ indicated; adding a `d5k`
   intersection ⇒ measured; `lab` alone finds nothing.
6. **Ledger** — a found entry survives a survey that would no longer detect it while the blurred
   value stays above the hysteresis floor; it is retired below it; a simulated anchor change
   (rewriting a column's `body` to a nearby direction) re-associates by class + proximity and keeps
   the confidence.
7. **Coverage bitmap** and the surveyed-cell counter.

`tests/deposits.js` (new, and extended in 0.6.1) pins the 0.6.0 part of the catalogue: anchor
quantization, id stability across 1000 rigid frames, the three thresholds, the cache invalidating
on a state signature change and not on a re-click.

`tests/gui.js` (extended): the seven checkboxes exist and prefill from `?inst=`; a click with an
empty set leaves the probe text untouched and surveys nothing; a click with `d5k` checked fills
`#prospect` and increments the ledger; the overlay draws and hides in 3D; `?depmap=0` suppresses
it; the capture line appears only when non-default. This needs the one test-harness change of the
increment: `tests/dom-stub.js`'s 2D context currently offers only `createImageData`/`putImageData`,
so it gains a recorded overlay surface (`clearRect`, `fillRect`, `beginPath`/`moveTo`/`lineTo`/
`closePath`/`fill`/`stroke`, `globalAlpha`, `fillStyle`, `strokeStyle`, and a counter plus the last
path) — the same "smallest DOM ui.js actually uses" policy the stub already states.

`tests/browser-scripts.js` (extended): the new script tags (`deposits` and `instruments` here,
`core` in 0.6.2) load in order under the vm context, and a scripted survey round-trips through
`Instruments.json`.

## 7. Order and acceptance

Order: `MapView.project` → `js/deposits.js` skeleton (anchor, id, thresholds, cache) →
`js/instruments.js` + ledger → UI panel + overlay → tests. The catalogue's economic fields are
filled by 0.6.1 without changing any signature here.

Acceptance:

1. **No default change.** With no instrument checked the page's behaviour, pixels and tests are
   identical to the pre-0.6.0 commit; the short profile stays green and the GUI pins unchanged.
2. **Same question, same answer.** Surveying one cell twice at the same state, from two different
   frames, prints identical lines; reloading the page and re-surveying from the same checkpoint
   prints identical lines.
3. **Drilling is decisive.** The same cell with `obs,geo,mag,gpr` reports readings and at most
   `inferred` entries; adding `d5k` can raise them to `measured` and print assay — and a `d500`
   hole on a 1.8 km-deep record finds nothing.
4. **No per-frame cost.** With an instrument set checked but no click, the frame time at L5 is
   within noise of the pre-change benchmark; the catalogue build never runs inside the frame loop
   (grep: no `Deposits.build` call from `frame()`), and the overlay redraws only on redraw.
5. **The overlay is bounded.** `#markers` clears and redraws in under 2 ms at L5 with 4000 markers
   on this sandbox, and the 3D toggle hides it without touching the camera or the session.
6. **The ledger is not state.** `Checkpoint.save` output for a world before and after a survey is
   byte-identical.
