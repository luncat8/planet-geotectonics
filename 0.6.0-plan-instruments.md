# 0.6.0 — Instruments and the survey click

The first increment of the deposit prospector (`0.6.x-plan-deposit-prospector.md`). Its primary
loop is local: select instruments, click one cell, read what those tools can measure there, and keep
that survey beside the ordinary pointer-follow column probe. Hover never surveys and nothing scans
the planet unless a later, explicit campaign asks it to. This matches the normal interaction — one
cell at a time — without making a regional map the default.

`js/deposits.js` supplies local candidate primitives: the column-frame anchor, stable id, thresholds,
quantized depth and one-cell blurred value. It does not build or cache a whole-planet catalogue in
0.6.0; `0.6.1` owns that catalogue and its economics. A local record carries
`kind, cell, anchorKey, id, potential, host, ageMyr, epochMyr, lat, lon, plate, top` (depth below
the surface). *Size, grade and viability arrive in `0.6.1-plan-deposit-catalogue.md`*, and the
0.6.0 report says so instead of implying assay data it does not have. Deposit markers and regional
campaigns are deferred to `0.6.2`, once there is a viable catalogue to mark.

## 1. What an instrument is

An instrument is three numbers and two predicates, not a minigame:

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `reach` | the deepest top-of-deposit it can sense, metres below the surface (or below the seafloor on a wet cell) |
| `coverMax` | the greatest sediment cover it works through; above it the target is blind |
| `footprint` | how far from the clicked cell it senses: `0` = the clicked column only, `1`/`2` = that many rings, `-1` = the three-hop upstream catchment whose `low` paths drain into the clicked outlet |
| `kinds` | the bitmask of record classes it can respond to at all |
| `detect` | the signal-to-noise limit at which a record is *found* (see §3) |

### 1.1 The seven instruments

Order in the table is the order in the UI. `class` keys are `Diag.ORE_NAMES` plus `iron` (derived,
0.6.1).

| id | name | reach | coverMax | footprint | kinds | character |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `obs` | Field observation | 50 m | 5 m | 1 ring | all | cheap, qualitative; outcrop, structure, gossan, stream pans |
| `geo` | Stream-sediment geochemistry | 0 (surface) | 200 m | 3-hop upstream drainage | all | basin-average anomaly; smears, cannot resolve grade |
| `mag` | Gravity + magnetics | 3 km (depth-integrated) | ∞ | 2 rings | `mafic vms arc iron` | shape and depth-to-source, no assay |
| `gpr` | Shallow reflection / ground radar | 1 km | ∞ | 0 | `basin placer iron` | sediment stack, depth to basement, placer horizons |
| `d500` | Shallow drill 500 m | 500 m | ∞ | 0 | all | assay of the interval; the only cheap quantitative read |
| `d5k` | Deep drill 5 km | 5000 m | ∞ | 0 | all | full column + assay; feeds the core (0.6.2) |
| `lab` | Assay + isotopes | sample | ∞ | 0 | all | refines a record already found; upgrades confidence |

Notes that keep the set honest:

- `obs` is the only instrument that needs *exposure*: a deposit is visible to it only if
  `top ≤ 50 m` **and** the cell is land (`z > 0`) or a wet seabed with `hSed ≤ 5 m` (a modern
  VMS mound). Its reading is an index, not a grade.
- `geo` walks the reverse routing edges (`state.low[j] === c`) up to 3 hops from the clicked
  outlet, collecting upstream cells whose sediment drains into it. It averages their blurred
  potentials weighted by `mobile` load; the loaded-cell count is printed so the dilution is visible.
  This direction matters: a source in a neighbouring cell is detectable only when its drainage
  actually reaches the clicked outlet, not merely because it is nearby.
- `mag` uses a two-ring footprint and the mafic/VMS/arc source families. It reports the clicked
  column's `hMaf` and `damage`, detected source families, and the shallowest detected source in a
  250 m depth bucket. It is qualitative, not an assay.
- `gpr` and `mag` work through cover; `obs` and `geo` do not; `d500`/`d5k` are below it by
  definition and report the cover they passed through.
- `lab` is the only instrument that cannot find anything on its own. Selected alone on an
  unsurveyed cell it reports an empty reading, which is the honest answer. In 0.6.0 a known sample
  can gain one confidence level; its grade/isotope assay fields arrive with the 0.6.1 record.

### 1.2 Cost

There is no money in 0.6.x. The cost of an instrument is *information* — reach buys depth, assay
buys grade, footprint buys breadth, and only the two drills produce a quantitative number. The
0.6.0 ledger counts `surveyed cells` and `found records`; 0.6.1 can add viable counts when
size/grade exist. These are session-only reporting totals, not gating.

Rejected: simulated instrument time or a per-instrument cooldown. It would make the demo wait on a
timer to hide a cost model that does not exist yet; when the economy increment lands, the counter
is the hook it prices.

## 2. What a click reports

`mapClick` keeps its existing meaning — probe the column, with the same hover/follow behaviour —
and additionally runs `Instruments.survey` when at least one instrument is checked. Hover never
surveys. With nothing checked, clicks remain probe-only as before. The ordinary `#probe` may follow
the pointer, but the separate prospect report remains pinned to the last clicked cell; moving the
pointer cannot erase a survey or add ledger coverage.

The report (`<pre id="prospect">`, beside the ordinary column inspector, monospace and scrollable)
is filled in this order:

```
cell 4,812 · 43.1°N 12.7°E · continental · z 480 m · sediment 250 m · t 1500.0 Myr
readings
  obs  clicked column covered · structure belt · drainage pans 0.04 · placer 0.34
  geo  upstream basin 7 loaded cells · anomaly index · arc 0.34
  mag  hMaf 3.1 km · damage 0.22 · magnetic index 0.34 · arc 0.34 · depth to source 1250 m
  gpr  sediment 250 m · depth to basement 250 m · reflectors 1 · placer 0.34
  d500  hole 500 m · cover 250 m · assay pending · no intersections
  d5k  hole 5000 m · cover 250 m · assay pending · intersections #3f2a91c4
  lab  known sample #3f2a91c4 · no further confidence gain
found deposits (2)
  #3f2a91c4  arc  measured  top 1250 m  thick continental · d5k+lab · size / grade pending
  #b703c008  placer  inferred  top 250 m  sediment · obs · size / grade pending
session 1 cells surveyed · 2 records found
```

Rules the panel obeys:

- **A reading is always printed; a record is only printed when found.** `readings` show the
  instrument's own number for every checked instrument, including the ones that see nothing (a
  barren cell is a result — `no reading above background`).
- **Drilling is the only way to reach measured confidence**, and only within the hole's reach.
  0.6.0 does not invent concentration numbers: quantitative grade/assay fields stay pending until
  the 0.6.1 catalogue supplies them.
- **A record found without a drill says so.** Any field the instrument cannot measure is `—` (or a
  range, when the instrument brackets it), never a false precision; 0.6.1 fills the slots.
- Values are quantized (S1) before they are printed: potentials `1/255`, depths 50 m, ages 1 Myr.
  The panel can therefore be compared line-for-line between two runs.

## 3. Detection: find, confidence, hysteresis

A record is a candidate for finding when it is inside the instrument's footprint and reach and
`cover ≤ coverMax`; the *finding* test is a deterministic signal-versus-noise comparison:

```
signal[k] = q255(potential) / 255 · sizeGain[k] · kindGain[instrument][kind]
noise     = Instruments.noise(seed, record.anchorKey, instrument)     // 0..1, integer hash
found     = signal ≥ detectLimit[instrument] · (0.7 + 0.6 · noise)
```

- `sizeGain` is `1` in 0.6.0 (no tonnage yet) and becomes the tonnage factor in 0.6.1; the shape of
  the test does not change, only the multiplier's source.
- `kindGain` is `1` for a favoured kind and `0.5` for a class the instrument only responds to
  indirectly (e.g. `mag` on an arc porphyry: the magnetic high is real but not diagnostic).
- `detectLimit` is per instrument and per kind family: `obs` 0.45, `geo` 0.25, `mag` 0.30,
  `gpr` 0.25, `d500`/`d5k` 0.10 (a drill that reaches a record *finds* it), `lab` — (never finds).

`Instruments.noise` mixes `(seed, anchorKey.x, anchorKey.y, anchorKey.z, instrumentId)` with the
integer avalanche mix `Contact.hash` uses, but keyed on the anchor and the instrument instead of
the cell and the frame. Same cell, same instrument, any frame: the same number. Two records in the
same cell differ because their anchors differ.

**Confidence** is the honest part of the report and rises only with evidence:

| level | label | requires |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `inferred` | remote sensing only (`obs`, `geo`, `mag`, `gpr`) |
| 2 | `indicated` | ≥ 2 independent instruments whose `found` tests both fired, or one instrument plus `lab` |
| 3 | `measured` | an intersecting `d500`/`d5k` hole (the assay brackets the interval), optionally refined by `lab` |

`lab` upgrades exactly one level per survey and never to `measured` without a drill. A record's
confidence never falls within a session (S5): once measured, the ledger keeps it measured for that
anchor.

**The ledger** (`Instruments.Ledger`, page-local, never checkpointed):

```
{
  coverage:   Uint8Array(grid.V),      // OR of instrument bitmasks used on each cell
  cells:      Uint32Array + cellsN,    // unique cells ever surveyed in this session
  found:      [ { id, kind, cell, anchorKey, direction, confidence, evidence, firstSeen } ],
  byId:       Object.create(null)      // stable id → found entry
}
```

- A `found` entry is written once. On every later survey its `confidence` is the maximum ever
  reached, its `cell` follows the record, and it is **retired only** when the record is gone *and*
  the cell's blurred potential has fallen below `0.7 × depositMin` — the hysteresis that stops a
  deposit flickering out of the player's notebook when the field crosses a bucket boundary.
- Re-association (S5): when a merge or terrane rebase changes an anchor, a stale entry is matched
  to the new record with the same `kind` whose world position is within 1.5 cells; on a match the
  entry's key and anchor are rewritten and the confidence carries over. No match ⇒ the entry is
  retired by the rule above.
- The ledger is rebuilt from nothing on reload and cannot be saved: exploration is a session
  activity, and the checkpoint format is untouched. (The deposits themselves are in the world; the
  next player can find them again — that is the point of S1–S3.)

## 4. Visual scope

0.6.0 does not add a marker canvas. The primary action is one cell click and the persistent report
already names that cell; drawing thousands of undiscovered candidates would turn a local question
into an implicit whole-map scan. The session ledger records where the user actually looked, but it
is not rendered as claims or a map layer in this increment.

`0.6.2` can add the opt-in viable map after the full catalogue exists. That increment owns the
bounded `#markers` canvas, view projection, visible-kind filter and 3D decision. Deferring it keeps
0.6.0 engine-neutral and avoids touching `js/render.js`, either renderer, or the parity harness for
a feature that is not needed to explore one clicked cell.

## 5. UI, URL, capture

- Add a fieldset `#prospect-tools` below Startup with the seven checkboxes (in the §1.1 order,
  `input name="inst"`) and the explicit hint “click a cell to survey it”. The separate `<pre
  id="prospect">` lives beside `#probe`; it has a fixed maximum height and scrolls instead of
  pushing controls down the page. The distinct ids are intentional: instrument results remain
  pinned while `#probe` follows the pointer.
- Prefill and capture follow the page's existing convention: `?inst=obs,geo,d5k` on load. The
  capture header gains `prospect inst obs,geo,d5k` only when at least one non-default instrument is
  selected (the default is an empty set). Markers, exports and the viable-map action belong to the
  catalogue/core increments, not the single-cell UI.
- Keep the existing `Deposits JSON` button (`Extract.json`, min 0.15, per-class 12) until 0.6.1
  can replace it with the versioned catalogue export. `js/extract.js` keeps its field primitives;
  removal of its legacy wrappers and the matching `tests/ores.js` change ship with 0.6.1.

## 6. Tests

`tests/instruments.js` (new, short profile; builds a small world, plants known columns, calls
`Instruments.survey` directly):

1. **Reach and cover gating** — a record at 800 m is invisible to `d500`, visible to `d5k`, and
   invisible to `obs`; a 300 m-deep placer under 400 m of sediment is invisible to `obs` and `geo`
   and visible to `gpr`/`d5k`.
2. **Footprint** — `obs` finds a record one ring away; `d500` does not; `geo` finds it only when
   the routing graph drains the record's cell into the clicked one. `mag` reports a detected
   source's depth in 250 m buckets. The placer rig's cone is reused to check that a pan signal
   appears downstream, not upstream.
3. **Determinism of noise** — same `(seed, anchor, instrument)` twice ⇒ identical; two anchors in
   one cell ⇒ different; the value does not change when `state.t` or `state.frame` changes.
4. **Detection limit** — a 0.2-potential record yields a reading but no `found` entry for `obs`; a
   0.9-potential one does.
5. **Confidence ladder** — `geo` alone ⇒ inferred; `geo`+`mag` ⇒ indicated; adding a `d5k`
   intersection ⇒ measured; `lab` alone finds nothing.
6. **Ledger** — a found entry survives a survey that would no longer detect it while the blurred
   value stays above the hysteresis floor; it is retired below it; a simulated anchor change
   (rewriting a column's `body` to a nearby direction) re-associates by class + proximity and keeps
   the confidence.
7. **Coverage bitmap** and the surveyed-cell counter.

`tests/deposits.js` (new, and extended in 0.6.1) pins the local candidate primitives: anchor
quantization, id stability across rigid frames, the trace/deposit thresholds, and the one-cell
blurred potential/peak rules. No whole-world build or catalogue cache is expected in 0.6.0.

`tests/gui.js` (extended): the seven checkboxes exist and prefill from `?inst=`; with an empty
set, a click still updates the ordinary column probe but leaves the prospect report and ledger
untouched; a click with `d5k` checked fills `#prospect` and increments the ledger; hover leaves that
report pinned; a captured GPU click keeps
its cell and selected instruments after `currentTarget` is cleared and a checkbox changes; the
capture line includes the selected instrument set. There is no overlay to draw or hide in 0.6.0.

`tests/browser-scripts.js` (extended): the new script tags (`deposits` and `instruments` here,
`core` in 0.6.2) load in order under the vm context, and a direct single-cell survey smoke test
returns a report with the selected cell; the UI pinning behavior is covered in `tests/gui.js`.

## 7. Order and acceptance

Order: `js/deposits.js` local candidates (anchor, id, thresholds and quantized potential) →
`js/instruments.js` + session ledger → pinned report and click wiring → focused tests. The
catalogue's economic fields are filled by 0.6.1; the viable-map overlay, if still useful, follows
in 0.6.2.

Acceptance:

1. **No default change.** With no instrument checked, the page's column-probe behaviour remains
   unchanged; hover never surveys, and the short profile stays green.
2. **One-cell-first, click-only.** One click evaluates only the selected instrument footprints
   around the clicked cell. The ordinary pointer probe can move without moving the report, and
   repeated hover does not change ledger coverage. There is no all-cell scan or marker map in this
   default workflow.
3. **Same question, same answer.** Surveying a cell twice at the same state, even from different
   frames, prints identical lines. Candidate identity is frame-stable; the session ledger survives
   re-clicks but is not saved to a checkpoint.
4. **Drilling is decisive.** Remote instruments can add inferred/indicated evidence; an intersecting
   drill can raise confidence to measured. A 500 m drill does not find a deposit whose top is deeper
   than its reach.
5. **No per-frame cost.** With instruments checked but no click, no survey work runs each frame and
   no global catalogue is built from the frame loop. The 0.6.0 overlay benchmark is deferred with
   the overlay itself.
6. **The ledger is not state.** `Checkpoint.save` output for a world before and after a survey is
   byte-identical.
