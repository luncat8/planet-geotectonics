# 0.6.1 review — the deposit catalogue and its economics

## 1. Review scope

The base is the 0.6.0 prospector: instruments, a click-only local survey, a session ledger and a
`Deposits.at` record that carried identity, host, depth and nothing numeric — the found line ended
in `size / grade pending`. This increment supplies the numbers, the class table they come from and
the viability screen, deletes the v1 `Extract` dump, and records the first measurement of the
whole thing.

The previous commit's own work was reviewed first and is sound: the drainage traversal, the
snapshotted GPU click and the magnetic source depth all hold up, and `Deposits.at` already had the
right shape for an economics layer to sit on. Nothing in 0.6.0 had to be undone. One 0.6.0 choice
*was* widened: the instruments only knew six deposit kinds, and `iron` is a seventh.

## 2. What the plan said and what the measurement changed

The plan was implemented as written except for five things, each of which was a measurement
result rather than a preference. All five are now in the plan itself (§2, §3, §10) so a
reimplementation does not rediscover them.

1. **Depth has to know its host.** The plan's rule was `top = emplacement + min(hSed, 4 km)` for
   every class. On the calibration history that buried every sediment-hosted class below its own
   mining depth: `orogenic/sedhost` 0 viable of 17, `basin/uranium` 1 of 38, `basin/potash` 1 of
   12, median tops of 4.2–5.2 km. But a placer, a coal seam, a roll front, a Carlin orebody and a
   Superior-type iron formation *are* the sediment pile — the pile is not on top of them. Each
   class row now carries `hosted: 'basement' | 'sediment'`, and only basement rows add the cover.
   After the fix those three classes read 3/17, 32/38 and 12/12, with median tops of 600, 380 and
   1,225 m.
2. **The potentials do not share a scale.** `oPla` is a transported load, not a saturating
   accumulator: its blurred field tops out at 0.144 on the 1500 Myr L5 history, so against the
   common `depositMin = 0.30` the placer class produced **zero** records and the placer half of
   the prospector was dead code. Each kind now carries a measured `FIELD_SCALE` that maps its own
   field onto the shared 0..1 deposit scale before quantization — `1` everywhere except `placer`
   (`7`, from the measured maximum). Placer now yields 96 records, 40 viable, at a viable median
   grade of 0.16 g/t Au, inside the plan's own 0.05–0.2 target.
3. **Iron needs both of design §8's settings.** The plan implemented only the exposure rule
   (`hFel < 2 km`, `z > 0`). The calibration history has **one** such cell: on a water-covered
   planet, exposed mafic crust above sea level is almost nonexistent, and the rule produced one
   iron record for the whole world. Design §8 also derives iron "from `oBas` in old basins", which
   is Superior-type BIF — the setting that supplies most of Earth's iron. The class now has two
   rows: `bif` on the old-basin rule (`hSed ≥ 2 km`, `age > 500 Myr`, sediment-hosted, because the
   formation is the basin fill) and `algoma` on the exposure rule (basement-hosted, smaller
   ladder). 38 and 1 records respectively, which is the right shape.
4. **Two class-table numbers did not survive contact with their own sources.** `basin/uranium`'s
   ladder ran `50 / 500 / 3,000 / 15,000 / 50,000` t U₃O₈ with a 25 t economic floor, while the
   sources the plan cites put the *average roll front* at 9,500 t: the ladder's T50 was three
   times below the published mean and the floor was two orders below any commercial operation.
   The ladder is now `200 / 1,000 / 9,500 / 30,000 / 100,000` with a 500 t floor. `basin/coal`'s
   aspect band `0.0005–0.01` produced 40 m "seams" against a row that says 0.5–8 m; it is now
   `0.0002–0.003`, which lands 2–20 m.
5. **The arc variant split was not exclusive.** `epithermal needs hFel < hOro` (45 km) is true of
   essentially every arc, so both arc rows qualified almost everywhere and the variant became a
   coin flip that no test could pin. The split is now `hFel < hOceanic` — the island-arc /
   continental-arc line — which is both the physical distinction and an exclusive one. The same
   treatment was applied to `mafic` and `orogenic`, where the plan already said "otherwise": only
   the three `basin` rows genuinely overlap, and only they are picked by hash.

Two smaller corrections: the plan's §3.1 worked example labelled `q = 107` as the `[T10, T50]`
band while its own §3.3 rule puts it in `[min, T10]`; the example is now the implementation's
actual output, verified number by number. And the example's conclusion is more interesting than
the plan's was — a stronger anomaly on the same column is a *bigger* deposit at the *same* grade,
because grade rides the anchor and only the ladder segment rides `q`.

## 3. What shipped

- `js/deposits.js`: `CLASSES` (13 rows), `FIELD_SCALE`, the quantizers (`snap125`, two-significant-
  figure grades, 50 m depth buckets, 1/1024 body shares), variant contexts, `at` (local),
  `build` / `atCell` / `viable` / `summary` / `stale` / `json` / `release` (explicit, cached on
  `(state, frame, V)`), and the derived iron field.
- `js/extract.js`: `deposits` and `json` deleted; `blur`, `host`, `peaks`, `compare` kept as the
  field primitives they always were.
- `js/instruments.js`: seven kinds instead of six (iron is a magnetic target and a drill target,
  not a radar one), and the found line prints the depth interval, variant, commodity, tonnage,
  size class, grades with units, body count and viability verdict.
- `js/ui.js`: the Deposits button exports `Deposits.json` v2; the per-grid scratch is now owned by
  `Deposits.release()`.
- `experiments/deposit-calibration.js` + `experiments/logs/deposit-calibration-L5-1500.log`: the
  measurement behind §10.
- Tests: `tests/deposits.js` rewritten around a one-second synthetic world that reaches all
  thirteen rows; `tests/ores.js`, `tests/instruments.js` and `tests/browser-scripts.js` moved to
  the v2 catalogue.

## 4. The honest part of the calibration

Where the sim's field saturates (`vms`, `arc`) the median record's per-body tonnage lands on the
published single-deposit median: 1.55 vs 2 Mt for VMS, 147 vs 220 Mt for porphyry. Where the field
tops out near 0.7 (`orogenic`, `basin`) most records fall in the ladder's lowest segment, so the
*median* body sits below the published median and it is the *90th-percentile* body that reaches it
(sedhost 5.5 vs 7.1 Mt, potash 200 vs 392 Mt). `basin/uranium` is still 5× short at p90.

That is reported, not tuned away. Giving every weak field its own scale would have pushed the
record count past acceptance 1's ceiling, and a median anomaly producing a marginal prospect is
the behaviour the umbrella plan's §4 asks for. The §8.5 distribution check therefore lives in
`experiments/deposit-calibration.js`, where it is a measurement with a committed log, rather than
in the test suite as an assertion with a tolerance chosen to make it pass.

## 5. Verification

- `node tests/deposits.js` — PASS (7 s), including the spy that proves a seven-instrument survey
  performs **zero** world scans and the explicit build performs exactly one per kind.
- `node tests/instruments.js`, `tests/ores.js`, `tests/gui.js`, `tests/adj.js`,
  `tests/browser-scripts.js` — PASS.
- `node tests/run-all.js` — PASS, short profile.
- `grep -R -e 'Extract.deposits' -e 'Extract.json' js tests` — empty (acceptance 3).
- Build budget: 21 ms at L5, 31 ms at L6, 74 ms at L7 against targets of 25 and 200 ms.
- Checkpoint bytes identical across a full build and a survey.

The full and release profiles were not run; the sandbox guidance reserves those histories for the
owner rig. The browser paths (the Deposits button's download, the panel in a real DOM) are covered
by the DOM-stub GUI test, not by a real browser.

## 6. Deferred

0.6.2 is unchanged by this increment and still owns the stratigraphic core, the optional regional
campaign and the opt-in viable map; all three read the finished record and `Deposits.summary`
without changing either. Two hooks are recorded rather than faked: the denudation history of plan
§7.1, which would let erosion delete a shallow record, and a per-class field scale for
`basin/uranium` if its p90 body is ever wanted on the published roll-front average.
