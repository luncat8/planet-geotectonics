# 0.6.x — deterministic deposits and prospecting

Implementation plan for `0.6.x-deposit-prospector-draft.md`. CPU-first, on demand,
classic scripts, offline/file://, no change to the physical kernels in the first release.

## 1. Meaning of the data

`State` holds fertility and six dimensionless metallogenic potentials:
`oVms`, `oMaf`, `oArc`, `oOro`, `oBas`, `oPla`. They express favourability, not an
inventory of elemental mass. `Extract.deposits` currently reports ranked maxima of
one-cell-blurred potentials. Its `depth = -s.z[cell]` is negative surface altitude,
not burial depth; never reuse it as drill depth. Local maxima and cell IDs are not
persistent deposit identities: peaks move with sampling, and columns compact.

Separate three layers:
1. **Geology snapshot:** immutable coherent inputs at one simulation time.
2. **Synthetic deposit catalogue:** deterministic bodies conditioned on that snapshot,
   with optional separately attributed Earth observations.
3. **Survey knowledge:** instrument results, confidence and discovery state. It does
   not reveal hidden catalogue fields or change physical simulation RNG/state.

UI/export must distinguish model potential, synthetic grade and observed assay.
None of these are certified reserves. No economic claim is a feasibility study.

## 2. First release boundary: a frozen exploration scenario

0.6.0 creates an explicitly frozen exploration scenario from a paused world. Save its
seed, generator/config version, source-pack identity, snapshot checksum, epoch and
catalogue together. Inspection, repeated clicks, rendering and survey order cannot
reroll bodies. Resume the simulation only after leaving exploration; preserve the old
scenario for revisiting/export and mark it as a historical snapshot, not a live map.
This satisfies stable calls and stable exploration sessions without pretending to
track ore through millions of years of deformation. Live geological evolution is 0.6.3.

On WebGPU, pause and drain the queue, then use one coherent authoritative readback;
CPU mirrors may be stale. Cancel safely on reset/device loss/world replacement.
Never trigger readback on hover or every animation frame. CPU/GPU physical runs can
differ numerically; deterministic catalogue equality is required for identical frozen
inputs, not promised for divergent simulations.

## 3. Generation and identity

Add `js/deposits.js`: pure generator/query routines, guarded Node export. Use an
independent integer hash/counter PRNG keyed by scenario seed, generator version,
fixed exploration-tile ID, deposit family and candidate ordinal. Freeze the tile grid
resolution in the versioned configuration, independent of render and sim grid level.
Use stable triangle/barycentric anchors (not lat/lon rounded display text). Sample the
snapshot's potentials/host fields onto these anchors once.

Generate a finite bounded set of candidates per tile. Acceptance probability and body
parameters depend on the family prior and local favourability; changing a potential
must not reroll unrelated candidate geometry. A candidate belongs to exactly one tile
by its anchor. Query a spatial index including bodies overlapping neighbouring tiles.
The per-tile catalogue is identical whether reached by a click or by whole-world scan.
RNG must not depend on iteration order, camera, frame count or instrument choices.

Record ID, family, commodities, anchor, orientation, host, top burial depth below
solid surface (m), thickness/axes (m), volume model, rock density (t/m³), ore fraction,
grade and unit, geological age range, confidence and provenance. Store water depth
separately; deposit top altitude = solid-surface altitude − burial depth. Do not derive
formation age from oceanic crust age or snapshot time alone.

Use one body/intersection model for maps, drill cores and resources: an ellipsoid is
sufficient initially. Validate positive dimensions, burial/host bounds, finite grade
and density. One poly-metallic body can carry several commodity grades; do not count
it as separate rock tonnage for each commodity.

Initial families and commodity candidates:

| Potential | Synthetic family | Initial commodities | Important constraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VMS | volcanic-hosted massive sulphide | Cu, Zn | oceanic/volcanic host |
| Mafic | mafic intrusion sulphide | Ni, Cu | needs suitable mafic host; not all mafic crust |
| Arc | porphyry system | Cu, Au | arc favourability is not a measured porphyry |
| Orogenic | lode system | Au | favourable belt and host |
| Basin | sediment-hosted system | Pb, Zn | not an automatic oil/gas or coal model |
| Placer | near-surface concentration | Au | shallow/unconsolidated host; geomorphology is simplified |

Begin calibration with VMS, porphyry Cu and orogenic Au; enable the remaining families
only with explicit priors and tests. Oil/gas, coal, bauxite, REE and uranium need host,
chemistry or thermal-history variables not presently represented and stay out of scope.

## 4. Calibration, quantities and economics

Put versioned family priors in `js/data/deposit-models.js`, with a source manifest under
`data/deposits/`. Before choosing numeric ranges, extract published/licensed deposit
model grade–tonnage distributions: source, sample population, units, correlations,
truncation and uncertainty. Do not draw independent grade and size if the chosen
model specifies dependence. Assumptions unsupported by data must be labelled game
parameters. Modern Earth observations override only their documented bodies, not
whole regions. Hold some districts out of calibration.

For an ellipsoidal ore envelope with semi-axes a,b,c:
- `volume_m3 = 4πabc/3`;
- `ore_t = volume_m3 × density_t_m3 × oreFraction` (no second occupancy multiplier);
- `metal_t = ore_t × grade_percent / 100`;
- for precious-metal grade in g/t, `metal_t = ore_t × grade_g_per_t / 1e6`;
- `recoverable_t = metal_t × recovery`.

Show estimated ranges, not false precision. These inferred resources do not consume
or conserve the sim potentials; mass conservation remains a physical-kernel concept.

A separate versioned **economic scenario** stores currency/year, commodity prices,
recovery/payability, mining/processing costs, depth/access penalties, capex and cutoff
assumptions. First screen: recovered payable value minus variable cost, plus a minimum
scale/capex proxy. Call it **scenario-positive estimate**, not commercially proven.
Depth is burial, not altitude; water/access penalties remain explicit. Changing price
or cutoff changes the filter, never the deposit or its geological ID. Do not suggest
NPV without a schedule and discount model.

## 5. Instruments: observations, not omniscience

`js/prospector.js` owns survey settings, intersections and deterministic measurement
noise. Survey keys include scenario, site anchor, instrument configuration and repeat
index; repeating the same saved survey returns the same result. Explicit repeat surveys
may improve confidence at a cost, but cannot endlessly reroll until a hidden body appears.

| Checkbox/tool | Response | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visual/outcrop | exposed lithology, alteration and visible mineral indicators | cannot assay concealed deposits; exposure and water cover matter |
| Surface sampling + assay | sampled surface material grade with units/uncertainty | local support, not whole-body tonnage |
| Drill 500 m | vertical core intersections from solid surface to 500 m | local borehole only; no discovery unless it intersects the body |
| Drill 5 km | same model to 5000 m, with larger cost | does not reveal every body within a 5 km radius |
| Magnetic/EM survey | synthetic anomaly/class confidence over a footprint | non-unique, host-dependent; no direct elemental concentration |
| Seismic survey | interpreted interfaces and structural depth uncertainty | not an assay or guaranteed ore detector |

Do not market ground-penetrating radar as kilometre-deep ore detection. Defer it until
near-surface material-dependent attenuation is modelled. Numerical sensitivity, false
positive rate and costs are explicit calibrated or game settings, not real instrument
specifications. A null result means not detected within survey support, not absent.

Checkbox combinations union observations with their provenance; they never expose the
hidden ground-truth panel. Keep that panel behind a labelled demo/debug reveal option.
Ocean drilling uses a distinct access scenario; do not count water as drilled rock.

## 6. Core and whole-world map

A core log shows depth interval, simplified sediment/felsic/mafic host and intersected
synthetic ore intervals with grades/uncertainties. Existing thickness aggregates are
not a historical stratigraphic column. Label the result **synthetic borehole log**;
no invented formation names or exact depositional ages. Interval lengths must sum to
the requested drilled rock length, with beyond-host data marked unknown.

Provide two separate map actions:
- **Discovered prospects:** only accumulated survey knowledge, with scenario filter.
- **Reveal modelled prospects:** explicitly omniscient demo/game-generation export of
  all catalogue bodies passing the economic scenario. Does not mark them discovered.

Whole-world generation runs in deterministic chunks with progress/cancel and a measured
memory cap; partial output is not published as complete. A reset invalidates pending
jobs. Export JSON includes configuration, units, source snapshot, provenance, confidence,
survey support and discovery state. Use one catalogue/query path for map and clicks.

## 7. Incremental implementation and gates

### 0.6.0 — deterministic catalogue MVP

Implement snapshot contract, pure generator, three calibrated families, spatial queries,
versioned export/import and a debug catalogue view. Keep `Extract` potential maxima as
an explicitly named diagnostic until the new catalogue UI replaces its role; do not
silently reinterpret the old JSON format's depth or value. Wire classic script load order
in the page and test harness; add Node tests to the short profile.

Gate: same snapshot/config gives byte-identical sorted catalogue across click order,
full scan, import and reload; no simulation RNG/checksum mutation; bounded grades,
hosts and units; seam/pole queries; zero duplicates at tile edges; resource formula
fixtures including percent and g/t; generator-version mismatch rejected before mutation.

### 0.6.1 — survey and synthetic core UI

Add inspector checkboxes, survey ledger, observation-only results, drilling/core display
and persistence in the exploration export. Test blind/covered/exposed targets, missed
and successful intersections, shallow versus deep drill, no hidden-field leaks, gap
handling, save/reload and GPU snapshot cancellation. Use the same picking path in 2D
and 3D; camera movement must not change a pinned survey anchor.

### 0.6.2 — economic screening and map export

Add scenario editor, discovered/reveal map actions and cancellable whole-world jobs.
Gate: filter changes leave catalogue checksum fixed; full map and local queries agree;
save/load preserves survey state; chunk size/order does not change output. Record L5/L7
snapshot, build/query latency, catalogue size and peak memory. Set release limits from
these measurements before enabling whole-world generation by default. Device timings
and browser file:// behaviour require owner-rig checks, not a stub assertion.

### 0.6.3 — optional live deposits (separate design gate)

Only proceed after stable identity is implemented across **all** lifecycle paths:
column birth, compaction, plate split/merge, burial/erosion, consumption and checkpoint.
Array indices, current grid cell and current plate number are not identities. Add a
persistent birth ID and event ordinal, migrate CPU/GPU layouts/readbacks and checkpoint
schema together; no save format back-compat is required. Follow deposits through
lineage/events, rather than reseeding each frame. Define transfer versus destruction
rules before tests; historical reconstruction is not reverse physical simulation.

Gate: tracked fixtures survive compaction/rigid motion with identical IDs, split/merge
ownership changes are deterministic, subducted deposits retire explicitly, and
checkpoint replay preserves the catalogue and knowledge. Regridding (0.7.0) needs an
explicit anchor/lineage mapping and must not reroll an existing exploration scenario.
Full history/parity and resource performance are owner-rig gates for this increment.
