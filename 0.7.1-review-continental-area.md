# 0.7.1 review — the continental-area gate, its seed spread, and what moved the level

## 1. Scope

`run_full_test.py` on the owner rig, 2026-10-13 08:55 (`experiments/logs/full-test-2026-10-13-08-55.log`),
is the first full profile since 2026-09-17. Everything is green up to

```
AssertionError [ERR_ASSERTION]: continental area 0.12434746293589476
    at Object.<anonymous> (tests\longrun.js:112:8)
FAIL longrun after 121783 ms
```

`tests/longrun.js:112` is acceptance 3 (`0.1.5-final-design.md` §10.3: *Hot start, 1500 Myr:
continental area 15–40 %, cratons present*). The design fixes that band, so the number is not
mine to redefine. The other two owner-rig logs in the same batch are green: `gpu-parity` (1000
frames, seeds 7/8/9, all within the predeclared bounds) and `webgpu-smoke` (PASS, 0 errors).

The last green full profile is 2026-09-17 04:16, on `acd7b8d`. Everything between then and
`8dfbdf1` is suspect: the 0.5.0 sediment mechanics (64688c8, 2140547), f32 elevation (6260e18),
and the 0.6.x/0.7.0 features that do not touch the kernel.

This review separates two different things that the single failing number collapses:

- **a gate defect** — the band is read on one seed of a statistic whose seed spread is wider
  than the miss;
- **a level change** — the model's standing felsic crust is genuinely ~20 % lower than it was,
  and that is a consequence of a correct fix, not of a bug.

## 2. What was measured

All numbers below are the L5 hot start at dt 0.1 that `tests/longrun.js` runs, reproduced in
`experiments/continental-probe.js`. The probe reproduces the failing log exactly (t=100: 19
plates, 9308 columns, 6.6 % continental, 12.64 cm/yr, 5 splits / 2 merges).

### 2.1 The statistic has a 7-point seed spread at the acceptance epoch

Three seeds at the current `HEAD`, 1500 Myr:

| seed | continental | cratons | mean hFel | mean hSed | middle-epoch speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | 12.4 % | 4.59 % | 6.31 km | 0.72 km | 9.57 cm/yr |
| 8 | 19.4 % | 8.35 % | 9.35 km | 1.37 km | 8.91 cm/yr |
| 11 | 16.0 % | 6.44 % | 7.87 km | 0.92 km | 8.87 cm/yr |

Continental area spreads over **7.0 points**; the middle-epoch speed over **0.70 cm/yr**. The
gate's miss is 2.6 points (0.1243 against a 0.15 floor) — *smaller than the spread of the
statistic it reads*. Seed 7 is the low tail, not a broken world: it has 89 splits and 91
merges, 4.6 % cratons, exact felsic and mafic ledgers, and passes every invariant in the file.

A single seed cannot decide acceptance 3. That is the defect that turned the suite red.

(While checking that table: the "middle epoch" window is `t >= 500 && t <= 1000` against a clock
that accumulates `t += 0.1` fifteen thousand times, so it reads 500.0000000000452 … 1000.0000000001588
— the t=1000 epoch is excluded and the window is really 500–900 Myr, five samples. It changes
the reported speed by 0.4 cm/yr and no verdict, so it is recorded here rather than quietly
changed, since moving an acceptance number is a decision of its own.)

### 2.2 It is not only seed luck: every seed dropped

The same probe against `acd7b8d`, the last state with a green longrun:

| seed | `acd7b8d` | `HEAD` | change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | 19.3 % | 12.4 % | −6.9 |
| 8 | 23.3 % | 19.4 % | −3.9 |

Mean 21.3 % → 15.9 %. So there is a real ~5-point level change underneath the spread, and what
the gate lost is margin: the ensemble mean now sits **0.9 points above the design floor**, where
the single seed it reads used to sit 4–8 points above it.

### 2.3 The mechanism: the sediment blanket, not the production

The felsic budget as global-equivalent thickness (seed 8, `acd7b8d` → `HEAD`):

| | gross arc production | felsic erosion | standing crust | standing sediment | sediment to the mantle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `acd7b8d` | 42.38 km | 30.61 km | 11.77 km | 8.18 km | 22.64 km |
| `HEAD` | 41.98 km | 32.63 km | 9.35 km | 1.37 km | 31.26 km |

Gross arc production is **unchanged** (the `producedFel` ledger is debited by felsic erosion, so
gross production is `producedFel + erodedFel`). What changed is how much of it survives: the
standing sediment fell 8.18 → 1.37 km, felsic erosion rose 30.61 → 32.63 km, and the standing
crust fell 21 %.

Erosion takes sediment first, then felsic, then mafic (`js/surface.js`, `route`). The old field
carried 6–8 km of sediment per column — the runaway-pile artifact the 3D view exposed, with
9–17 % of columns above the collapse regime — and that blanket absorbed the intake before it
ever reached bedrock. The accommodation limit (64688c8, 2140547) removed the artifact, and with
it the shield. The model now erodes bare crust, so more of the same production is lost.

The old 19–23 % was therefore **measured on an artifact**. Restoring it by restoring the artifact
is not available, so §2.4 asks whether the one calibration the field invalidated can buy it back.

### 2.4 Three candidates, measured: none of them moves the level

Both were run as 3-seed ensembles, because §2.1 makes a single seed uninterpretable.

| variant | seed 7 | seed 8 | seed 11 | mean | spread |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| shipped | 12.4 % | 19.4 % | 16.0 % | 15.9 % | 7.0 |
| barrier on `hFel` only (design §6 form) | 14.6 % | 15.8 % | 17.0 % | 15.8 % | 2.4 |
| filled basins bypass instead of draining to the mantle | 14.2 % | 18.0 % | 12.7 % | 15.0 % | 5.3 |
| `kEro` 0.042 (−16 % erosion intake) | 16.1 % | 18.4 % | 14.8 % | 16.4 % | 3.6 |

- **The collision barrier.** Design §6 grows the barrier with *continental hFel*; 2140547 grew
  it with `hFel + hSed`, so a sediment-loaded margin goes hard. Reverting to the design's form
  is worth +2.2 points on seed 7 and −3.6 on seed 8: the mean does not move. What it does move
  is the spread, 7.0 → 2.4, which is a real observation and is recorded rather than shipped —
  three seeds estimate a spread badly, and "it makes the number I read steadier" is not a
  reason to change a force law.
- **The basin bypass.** A column at the accommodation limit books everything it refuses to
  `subductedSed`: a filled basin is a mantle sink. That has no physical justification — a real
  basin spills over its lowest rim — and it is not a small term, ~8.6 km of global-equivalent
  sediment over 1500 Myr, about a quarter of all the felsic the arcs ever made. Making the
  refused load stay mobile and spill downhill doubles the standing sediment (0.72 → 1.49 km on
  seed 7, closer to Earth's ~0.5 km global mean than either 0.72 or 8.18) and cuts felsic
  erosion, but the ensemble mean moved the *wrong* way (15.9 → 15.0), and seed 11 fell 16.0 →
  12.7. Not shipped: no measured benefit, and the dead-zone waiver it needs is a threshold test
  on a quantity both engines pin at the cap, which is exactly the f32/f64 razor tie the
  GPU-parity gate exists to catch. It is written up here so the decision is cheap for whoever
  picks it up, with the hazard already identified.

- **The erosion intake.** `kEro` −16 % is worth +3.7 points on seed 7 and −1.0 on seed 8: the
  ensemble mean moves 15.9 → 16.4 %, half a point, and the spread halves to 3.6. So the level is
  not primarily set by the erosion rate either — extra crust lands on every column, and the
  statistic counts only the columns that cross 20 km.

No candidate restores the ~5 points of §2.2. Four variants, three seeds each, and every ensemble
mean lands in 15.0–16.4 %. The level is not a knob any of these reaches.

## 3. What was built

### 3.1 `experiments/continental-probe.js`

The acceptance history with the columns the gate needs and the ones that explain it: per-epoch
continental share, cratons, mean hFel and hSed, the share of columns above the collapse regime,
and the felsic budget split into gross production, cumulative felsic erosion, standing crust,
standing sediment and sediment sent to the mantle. Each of the three 0.5.0 sediment changes is
a switch on the probe (`--barrier=fel`, `--cap=off`, `--sedflow=off`) rather than an edit of
`js/`, so a candidate is measured by running the shipped code paths, not a copy of them.

### 3.2 `experiments/erosion-knee.js` can rank a candidate on an ensemble

Its seed was pinned to 7, so the one harness the findings point at for an erosion candidate
could not do the thing §2.1 says is mandatory. It is now a `--seed` flag. Its sediment column
also printed metres under a `km` label (`sed 752.67 km` for 752.67 m); the numbers quoted here
are the corrected ones.

### 3.3 The acceptance gate is now an ensemble

`tests/longrun.js` still runs seed 7 as the primary history — it carries the core/catalogue
check, the dt-0.01 cross-check, the checkpoint ring and the per-epoch invariants. Acceptance 3
is now read on a three-seed ensemble (seeds 7, 8, 11) with two predeclared bounds:

- the **mean** continental area is inside the design band (15–40 %);
- the **spread** is inside a declared window (0.12; measured 0.024–0.070 across the four
  variants in §2.4). Three seeds estimate a spread badly, so the bound is a guard against a
  model that turns wildly seed-sensitive, not a second acceptance band.

Every seed is printed. The spread bound is what keeps the ensemble honest: a model that became
wildly seed-sensitive fails even if its mean is comfortably in band. Cratons are still gated per
seed (every seed has them, 4.6–8.4 %).

What this changes and what it does not: it stops the suite from reading one chaotic world as the
model. It does **not** restore the margin the model used to have — §2.2.

## 4. Open decisions (not decided here)

1. **The margin, and whether it is a calibration question at all.** `kEro = 0.05` / `zKnee = 9000`
   were measured against acceptance 3 on the shielded field (findings, "the erosion knee is a
   release calibration, not a cosmetic constant": one degree of freedom, `kEro·z³/zKnee²`). The
   field changed under them — but §2.4 measures `kEro` −16 % at **+0.5 points** on the ensemble,
   so re-calibrating erosion does not buy the margin back. If the ~5 points matter, the question
   is not the constant: it is whether arc production (`kArc`, unchanged across every variant
   here, ~42 km of global-equivalent felsic per 1500 Myr) is the right size, or whether the
   design's 15 % floor was written for the model the shielded field produced. That is the
   design author's and the owner's call, not a test to edit.
2. **The design floor itself.** The band is 15–40 %; Earth is ~30–40 %. The model was always
   felsic-poor (21 % before the sediment fix, ~16 % now) and the floor was written to
   accommodate that. If ~16 % is the model working as designed, the floor is the thing to
   revisit — but that is the design's author's call, not a test to edit.
3. **The basin bypass** (§2.4): physically right, measurably neutral here, and it needs a
   GPU-mirror change with an identified f32/f64 tie hazard.
