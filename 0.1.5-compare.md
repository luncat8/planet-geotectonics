# 0.1.5 — Comparison of the three tectonics designs

Inputs: `0.0-draft.txt` (requirements), `0.0-draft-check.txt` (ore mechanisms), the three
design documents `0.1-diz-gpt-5.2-high.md`, `0.1-diz-gemini-3.8-flash-high.md`,
`0.1-diz-GLM5.3-flash.md`, the existing `js/geodesics.js` grid, and two hard constraints
added after the designs were written:

- resolution: **L5 on CPU** (10,242 cells, ~223 km), **L6–L7 on GPU** (41k–164k cells, 112–56 km);
- time resolution: **10k–100k years per frame** (dt = 0.01–0.1 Myr);
- implementation order: CPU (plain JS) first, then WebGPU.

The merged result is `0.1.5-final-design.md`; the plan built on it is `0.2-plan.md`.
Measurements quoted below come from `experiments/advection-bench.js` (runnable with node).

---

## 1. The regime the designs must survive

Plate speeds are 1–10 cm/yr (up to ~20 on a hot planet) = 10–100 km/Myr. Per-frame
displacement at the requested dt:

| level | cells | spacing | 1 cm/yr, dt 0.01 | 5 cm/yr, dt 0.1 | 10 cm/yr, dt 0.1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L5 | 10,242 | 223 km | 0.0004 cell | 0.022 cell | 0.045 cell |
| L6 | 40,962 | 112 km | 0.0009 | 0.045 | 0.090 |
| L7 | 163,842 | 56 km | 0.0018 | 0.090 | 0.179 |

Consequences that none of the three designs was written for:

1. Motion is **sub-cell every frame**. A cell-to-cell copy scheme has nothing to copy
   for tens to hundreds of frames, and 10^4–10^5 frames accumulate over a planet history.
2. Anything that re-samples a field per frame will be applied 10^4–10^5 times. Numerical
   diffusion per application must be *zero*, not merely small.
3. The per-frame cost is dominated by O(V) cell passes, not by transport; that is good for
   the GPU and fine for L5 on the CPU.

All three designs assumed dt sized to move ~0.5 cell per step (GPT: 0.5–2 Myr, Gemini:
0.1–0.5 Myr, GLM: CFL 0.5 → 1–2 Myr at L5). That is 5–200× coarser than required.

## 2. Measured evidence

`node experiments/advection-bench.js 5 0.1 1000 5` — a rigid 30° cap ("continent")
rotates at 5 cm/yr for 1000 Myr (50,000 km travelled, ~2 laps). IoU = overlap of the
simulated cap with the exactly rotated cap.

| scheme | proposed by | cells 669 → | IoU | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| semi-Lagrangian nearest cell, raster when moved 0.5 cell | GPT §6.1 | 669 | 0.00 | 97 % of cells map back to themselves: **the plate never moves** |
| same, raster at 0.7 cell | GPT | 0 | 0.00 | nearest-cell map is not a bijection on an irregular Voronoi grid: the plate **erodes to nothing** |
| same, raster at 1.0 cell | GPT | 605 | 0.11 | moves but distorts; 200 rasters ≈ one lap |
| Eulerian upwind flux, every frame | GLM P7, Gemini §7 | 106 (h>0.5) | 0.16 | mass exact; 10–90 % edge width **5,800 km = 26 cells** |
| Lagrangian columns, exact rotation, nearest-column raster every frame | (GPT's deferred "variant B", done properly) | 695 | **0.97** | zero interior holes, exact mass, 3 ms/frame at L5 |

`node experiments/advection-bench.js 5 0.01 100 5` (10 kyr frames, 5,000 km): upwind
already smears to 11 cells (2,500 km); semi-Lagrangian at 1.0 cell reaches 0.79 only because
there were just 22 rasters; columns 0.97. The upwind smear follows √(dx·L) and therefore
does **not** improve with smaller dt; at L7 it is still ~24 cells per ocean crossing.

Other checks made on the actual grid:

- Nearest-column distance from a cell centre, 1 column per cell, rigid rotation, 3000 frames:
  p50 0.37, p99.9 0.61, max 0.65 of the local neighbour distance (same at L5 and L6).
  A threshold of 0.75 cleanly separates "covered" from "genuine divergent gap".
  Occupancy stays 7 % / 86 % / 7 % for 0 / 1 / 2 columns per cell.
- `nbrB.rotA/rotB` is a proper rotation (|rot| = 1 to 4 decimals): Gemini's tangent-vector
  transport between neighbour bases is valid.
- GPT's mantle driver `u = Σ A_m (a_m × r)` equals `(Σ A_m a_m) × r` to 5e-16: it is one
  rigid rotation of the whole sphere. Zero divergence, zero shear, no differential forcing.
- CPU cost of rotate + hill-climb for 10k points: 3.2 ms/frame in node; ×16 at L7 → GPU
  needed there, as the constraint says.

## 4. Per-design notes

### GPT-5.2-high
Strong on structure: the A/B/C taxonomy, explicit plate objects with Euler poles, edge
classification, subduction polarity, event-based split/merge, ore extraction as local maxima,
and a pass list that can be implemented. Two defects are fatal as written: the mantle field
is a single rigid rotation (proved above), and semi-Lagrangian cell copying does not work at
sub-cell steps (measured). It also has no isostasy (elevation is bumped by rates, so
mountains have no self-limiting height) and no sediment. Its own suggestion to add tracers
later is the right idea; we make it primary.

### Gemini-3.8-flash-high
Physically the most honest: Airy isostasy from a felsic/mafic/sediment column, density from
thermal age, a real force balance, viscoplastic rheology, first-class erosion, a clear
metallogenic table and cooling epochs. The kinematics does not hold together: momentum
smoothing is not rigid-body motion (plate interiors keep shearing), the advection is a
per-frame re-sampling (diffusive), "bilinear sampling" of W×H-packed cell textures is
meaningless (texel neighbours are not spatial neighbours), and without plate identity there
is no polarity or trench location. Level→km numbers are off by 4×.

### GLM-5.3-flash
The most complete engineering document: budget table, pass I/O table, two-velocities-per-edge
convention, the fluid decision, determinism rule, invariants, acceptance probes, checkpoints,
risks. Its transport (conservative upwind) is exactly the thing that fails at the required dt,
and it knows it ("drift watchdog mandatory", MacCormack "optional"). Rigidity by periodic
clustering gives no persistent plate object — no stable Euler pole, no plate history, hard to
name/track plates in a game — and interiors drift between projections. Its ω least-squares
solve, fluid decision, isostasy calibration, ore closure, and test discipline are all kept.

## 6. Rejected

- Any per-frame Eulerian or semi-Lagrangian transport of crust fields (measured).
- Velocity smoothing as a rigidity model (not rigid).
- Rigid-rotation mantle driver (no differential motion).
- Transported fluid/metal phase (GLM's argument: sub-cell at 10–50 km).
- Full periodic table of element textures (draft): 6 potentials + context at extraction.
- L8/L9 targets, WebGL2 leftovers, atomics-free-at-all-costs (integer atomics are deterministic).
