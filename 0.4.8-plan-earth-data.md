# 0.4.8 — present-day sediment

Data-quality increment for the modern Earth packs. Not a claim of measured ore grades, and
not a new relief or plate model. It can proceed independently of 3D. Owner-rig Earth GPU
parity is still required before a release claim; this increment does not change a default.

## 1. What is already there

- `js/data/earth-1deg.js` and `js/data/earth-05deg.js` are the present-day packs. Sediment
  in a fresh paleo bake is a three-number prior, not a measurement: exposed land 2000 m,
  flooded shelf 6000 m, ocean `min(5000, 1000 + 4000·exp(-dist/4))` with dist in chebyshev
  cell steps. `kind` bit 4 marks that elevation/age prior. Do not reuse it as a sediment flag.
- `js/earth.js` packs sediment in 100 m steps (`scale.sed` 0.1 km). Decode inverts every cell
  with `Earth.invert` (five smoothstep iterations, continental age 500) and apply interpolates
  thicknesses, not z. The score compares the live elevation with the pack's z.
- `data/earth/calibration_points.json` is a physics fixture. It is not a sediment dataset.
  Do not fit it.
- GlobSed v3 (PANGAEA 982339, CC-BY 4.0) is the intended oceanic raster. It is not in the
  tree. Oceans in this increment are the CRUST1.0 fallback, labelled as such.
- Historical packs keep the prior. A sediment correction that is not epoch 0, or whose pack
  name is not `earth`, is refused.

## 2. Contract

Replace the sediment bank of the two modern packs with CRUST1.0 sedimentary thickness.
Leave relief, ocean age, plate ownership, the datum and `scale.sed` alone except for the
envelope rule below.

Sediment is the sum of the three sedimentary layers in `crust1.bnds` (boundary steps 2–4).
Water and ice are not `hSed`. The grid is 180×360, north-first, cell centres 89.5°N,
−179.5°E: `ilat = floor(90 − lat)`, `ilon = floor(180 + lon)`, longitude wraps, latitude
clamps. A 1° pack centre lands on that cell. A 0.5° pair shares the containing 1° cell.
No interpolation. Quantize to the pack's 100 m step, cap 25500 m. Zero in the model is
modelled zero, not an unknown.

Commit the compact extract `data/earth/sediment-crust1.bin` (`PGTSED01`, uint16 metres).
Do not commit `crust1.bnds`. The jrleeman tree is a Python/KMZ adaptation under MIT; that
licence does not cover the grids. Cite Laske, Masters, Ma and Pasyanos (2013).

Provenance is the pack source string (`+ CRUST1.0 sediment (not GlobSed)`) and
`data/earth/sediment-baseline.json`. Erosion overwrites `hSed` within a step, so the
inspector reports the live column. Do not add probe text that says the live thickness is
still the CRUST1.0 value.

### Envelope

For each cell, with the new sediment and the loader's own invert:

- if the derived elevation is within 1 m of the stored z, keep z;
- if the invert hits a thickness bound (oceanic `hMaf` 2000 or 35000 m, continental `hFel`
  1000 or 80000 m) and the elevation differs by more than 1 m, store the rounded clamped
  elevation;
- otherwise keep z and count the residual.

Continental invert always writes `hMaf = 0`. That is the branch, not a bound. The five-iteration
smoothstep does not close to 1 m on every transitional cell (residuals up to about 75 m), and
a distant integer that does close it rewrites the shelf. Do not search for that integer.
The clamped elevation round-trips; a second apply is then a fixed point and byte-identical.
Do not add iterations inside `Earth.invert` to hide the residual.

The datum search is not reopened. z moves only by this rule. Age, kind and the plate table
are not touched. The header wet fraction is recomputed from the stored z (cosine latitude).

On the committed prior packs this moves 1131 cells at 1° (2 sign flips, 348 residuals, max
75 m) and 4796 cells at 0.5° (11 sign flips, 1446 residuals, max 75 m). The L5 land mask
against the prior pack stays IoU 1, because the moves are oceanic. Those counts are the
check against `sediment-baseline.json`, not a target to hit by editing z by hand.

## 3. Point observations, later

`tools/earth/sediment.js` validates an observation table and can apply inverse-distance
weights inside an explicit radius. Same crust class only (`kind` bit 0). No support means
the labelled prior stays. Holdouts are not applied. z is frozen: sediment walks back toward
the prior, in 100 m steps, until the loader reproduces z. Unknown is null with a reason.
Zero is a measurement only when `uncertainty_m` is a number — `null >= 0` is true in JS, so
the check is by type. Duplicate ids, missing provenance and bad coordinates are rejected.

No observations file is shipped. Do not invent thicknesses to fill a map, and do not claim
a holdout win. A later file is `data/earth/observations.json`: id, lat, lon, field
`sediment_thickness`, metres, uncertainty, footprint, crust class, role
(`observed` / `interpreted` / `prior`), holdout, source id, dataset version, extraction,
record. The source registry carries licence, citation and the extraction command.

## 4. Bake and registry

`node tools/earth/sediment.js --extract-bnds <crust1.bnds> --out data/earth/sediment-crust1.bin`

`node tools/earth/sediment.js --apply --pack js/data/earth-1deg.js --sediment data/earth/sediment-crust1.bin`

The same for `earth-05deg.js`. Run `--apply` twice; the second write is byte-identical.
`--report` prints area-weighted sediment, wet fraction and the z/sed SHA-256. Reports are
not compared across runs if they grow a timestamp; the baseline JSON has none.

`bake_earth.py --sediment <bin>` is that post-pass, default off. It runs after the pack is
written. It refuses a non-`earth` or non-epoch-0 pack. It does not feed the extract into
the datum loop.

`data/earth/manifest.json` lists MD5s. The bake verifies a `--paleo`, `--plate-ids` or
`--sediment` path when it is listed, and refuses a mismatch. An unlisted path is not
checked. The paleo MD5s are the ones already published in `data/earth/SOURCES.md`; do not
replace them. The bake does not verify every dataset named in that file.

The committed modern plate table is a nearest-seed Voronoi of NNR-MORVEL56, not PB2002.
PB2002 remains a candidate that was not ingested.

## 5. Gates

- Sediment in both modern packs equals the quantized containing-cell extract. A second
  apply is byte-identical. Unbound residuals are counted and stay under 80 m. No residual
  sits on a thickness bound.
- Datum, age, kind and plates unchanged. Historical packs unchanged.
- `tests/earth.js` gates stay satisfied: wet fraction 0.7081±0.015, mean land 600–1000 m,
  mean ocean −4800 to −3800 m, round-trip RMS ≤ 250 m. `tests/paleo.js` land-mask IoU
  against the modern pack stays inside ±0.02. Short profile only in the sandbox
  (`node tests/run-all.js`). No full profile, no release profile.
- Startup-loaded bytes grow by the sediment-bank change inside the existing packs, not by
  a new runtime fetch. The extract is a bake input, not a page script.
- Do not describe the result as a better map, a GlobSed correction, or a holdout
  improvement. It replaces a three-number prior with a published 1° model, and says so.

## 6. Not this increment

Concentrations, grades, tonnage and discovery records. An occurrence is not the grade of
a geodesic cell. The 0.6.x plan owns that separation. A finer-than-100 m sediment channel,
a GlobSed ocean correction, and a point-observation fit are separate increments with their
own provenance. None of them reopens the datum search or the historical packs.
