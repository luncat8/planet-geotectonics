## What the review got wrong (checked against the code)

1. **"D1 is SOLVED — f32 vs f64 divergence, document and close."** False. The
   K10 witness only proves the device's relaxation arithmetic is
   self-consistent: `dt 0.1` (the uploaded value), `alpha 0.2`, worst
   `|stored − relaxed| 0.00e+0`. It recomputes the formula with the device's
   own `fit` and `ω_prev`, so it cannot see that the device's fit differs from
   the CPU's fit. The divergence is unexplained: mean ratio 0.837 (CPU) vs
   0.973 (device), per plate 0.691 vs 0.966 (p2) and 0.863 vs 1.167 (p4),
   worst `|dR·ω|` on plate 14 (the smoke's table prints only the first four
   plates). The mirror already ships everything needed to adjudicate — per
   plate `M`, `rhs`, `omegaTarget` (the fit), `omega`; per cell `uMantle`,
   `wEq` — and the smoke simply never compares them across engines. D1 stays
   OPEN. The 20% mean-speed gate stands. Do not close it, and do not widen
   the tolerance, on the strength of the review's story.
2. **"The Phase I4 readback fix is not in this build."** False. The offscreen
   path was in the build the whole time — `archive/0.3-2-log,task.txt` carries
   the identical 32 errors. The real bug: the offscreen texture's usage was
   `0x10 | 0x4` with a comment claiming `COPY_SRC`; `0x4` is *BufferUsage*'s
   COPY_SRC, *TextureUsage*'s is `0x1`. Dawn's error
   `(TextureBinding|RenderAttachment) doesn't include CopySrc` matches the
   buggy bits exactly, and the blit (which needs TEXTURE_BINDING) worked — the
   map drew, only the copy failed, so the final line read "pixel-parity
   checked" beside 16 × "readback short". The structural test defined its own
   `COPY_SRC = 0x4` and pinned the typo, and the stub validates nothing.
   **Fixed in tree this session**: `js/gpu/render-gpu.js` usage is now
   `0x10 | 0x4 | 0x1` (RENDER_ATTACHMENT | TEXTURE_BINDING | COPY_SRC) and
   `tests/gpu-readback.js` pins all three bits by name. Verify
   `node tests/gpu-readback.js` and move on; do not re-diagnose.
