06 / Metallogeny

Every million years the plate table is rebuilt: consumed columns are compacted, plates that stopped moving relative to a neighbour suture into it, plates that lost their last column disappear, and a damaged corridor that the mantle flow is pulling apart becomes a new plate with its own rotation.

Six saturating potentials accumulate where the geologic factory runs: VMS at ridges, mafic under plumes, arc behind trenches (enriched by whatever that plate is subducting), orogenic in collision belts, basin and placer where sediment lands. Deposit extraction blurs each field one cell and lists its ranked maxima with a context tag. The mantle cools on a 2.5 Gyr exponential, so flow, slab pull, ridge push and lithosphere strength all fade together. Hot starts have no continents: every craton in the map is built by arcs and collisions. Snapshots every 20 Myr can be saved to a file and loaded back.
