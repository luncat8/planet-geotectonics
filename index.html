03 / Crust cycle

Divergence opens gaps that spawn new columns — oceanic from the mantle, or thinned from the rift flanks. Convergence brings foreign columns inside contact range: the loser is consumed, its sediment scraped into an accretionary prism, and the overriding plate grows arc crust two cells behind the trench.

Slab pull, ridge push and collision resistance now drive the plates alongside basal drag. Surface erosion and plate split/merge are later phases.
