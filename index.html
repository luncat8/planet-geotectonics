The calibrated CPU path runs the complete L5 history with no build step. Choose a hot start and “Run to target” for the 4.5 Gyr stability history; the run uses the same deterministic frame kernels, checkpoints and layer renderer as interactive play.

Every million years the plate table is rebuilt: dead columns compact, quiet boundaries suture, and a damaged corridor pulled open by the mantle becomes a new plate. Six saturating potentials preserve the metallogenic record at ridges, plumes, arcs, collision belts and sediment basins. Static edge coefficients and bounded-vector kernels leave more of the frame budget for that coupled crust cycle, while the performance strip reports every kernel twice a second. L5 is the calibrated CPU resolution; L6 (40,962 cells) and L7 (163,842 cells) are the WebGPU path, and the Resolution select rebuilds the world at whichever level you pick.
